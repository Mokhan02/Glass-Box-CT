## 3. W1: training-data contrast audit

**Question.** Are liver tumours that are brighter than the surrounding liver rare among the public liver-tumour labels VISTA-3D was trained on, compared with the held-out HCC cohort?
**Pre-registration.** [PREREG.md](results/w1_training_audit/PREREG.md), committed 9e1a515. Decision rule: d = p_HCC - p_train (bright = tumour minus liver-shell contrast > 0 HU, two largest lesions >= 10 mm per case, pooled primary), margin 0.10, at least 200 training lesions; applied in order: BRIGHT RARE IN TRAINING (CI lower > 0 and d >= 0.10), REVERSED (CI upper < 0), NO GAP (whole CI within +/-0.10), INCONCLUSIVE.
**Named confounders:** contrast phase and tumour type (metastases vs HCC).

## 6. Open questions and limitations

- W1 cannot separate "labels rarely bright" from contrast phase and tumour type; W4 and W5 address phase.
- Which VISTA-3D training cases fell in the 64% training split is not public; W1 audits all labelled cases.
- VISTA-3D's MAISI synthetic-tumour stage is not covered by W1.
- C4KC-KiTS may be in-distribution at the image level, and AbdomenCT-1K's tumour labelling is unresolved.
- C4KC-KiTS is described as arterial phase; phase cannot be labelled on HCC-TACE-Seg.
- W1: the HCC reference is 75 cases (86 lesions); the 89-case sensitivity analysis and the optional Task08 secondary are not run; the contrast of a lesion is a coarse mean-HU summary.
- W2 v3: the C4KC-KiTS images may have been seen in training (via AbdomenCT-1K, tumour labelling unknown); the scans are arterial/corticomedullary phase; the dark-lesion validity gate was set after seeing the pilot; the kidney GAP point estimate (+10.3 pts) is just above the 0.10 margin; the kidney gap is mostly over-segmentation (leak) while the HCC gap is mostly under-segmentation, so "GAP" does not mean the same failure in the two organs; the HCC bright group has 23 lesions; no multiplicity correction across the three verdicts; point prompts give location, so this is not detection; mechanism steps (probe, LEACE, patching) are not run.
- W2-M route check: HCC and kidney differ in tumour type, phase mix, size and baseline Dice, so the absolute Dice drops are not like-for-like; both erasers are single linear directions at one layer; the positive control reproduces mech B's route, not its exact numbers.
- W2-M: the M3 Arm B manipulation was weak (median realised post-edit |contrast| about 8 HU), so its null does not show that darkening cannot remove the leak; M2 tested one linear direction at one layer (mid-encoder) with a seeded 60-case organ check; the kidney failure is mostly LEAK and its mechanism is not identified by M1-M3.
- W2 (earlier note): the class-plus-point prompt returned an organ-sized mask; whether a point-only (no class) prompt would segment the lesion is untested and would need sign-off and a new pre-registration. The point-prompt coordinate convention must pass a unit test before any scored run; whether HCC should use class 130 or class 26 with a point is a choice marked for approval; the kidney label spans cortex and medulla, which differ in the corticomedullary phase.
