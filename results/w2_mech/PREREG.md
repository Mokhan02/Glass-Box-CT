# Pre-registration W2-M: mechanism steps for the kidney result

Status: APPROVED (2026-10-18) with edits and committed on `research/roadmap` before any W2-M run. M1 runs first and stops for a report; M2 and M3 run only after M1 is reported and the researcher agrees. Deviations go to `results/w2_mech/DEVIATIONS.md`. **Inclusion or exclusion changes need the researcher's sign-off before they are applied, even pre-analysis.** Nothing here touches `main`.

## Context and question
W2 v3 (`results/w2_kidney/v3/`) found, on unseen main-set lesions, a Dice gap (bright segmented worse than dark) for kidney with the zero-shot point route (label 133, point label 1): +10.3 pts [+4.0, +17.6] (GAP, just above the 0.10 margin). The kidney failure is mainly LEAK (over-segmentation: 16% dark vs 40% bright), whereas the HCC failure was mainly missed tumour (UNDER). The HCC mechanism (`results/voxel_probe`, `results/mech`, `results/contrast_cf`) found: the mid-encoder represents missed tumour tissue; erasing the HCC tumour-vs-liver direction costs lesion Dice (-0.33); brightening dark tumours causes misses. Does the same encoder concept explain the kidney result?
- M1: is the HCC tumour concept shared with kidney tumour tissue?
- M2: does erasing the HCC-derived concept change kidney tumour segmentation (and spare the kidney organ)?
- M3: does brightening dark kidney tumours cause LEAK, and darkening bright ones remove it?

## Common to all (reused from the HCC work)
- **Model and layer:** frozen NV-Segment-CT (`vista3d132`). Mid-encoder = output of `image_encoder.encoder.layers[2]["blocks"]` (192 channels, 32^3 per 128^3 window), token = average-pooled x2, stride 8 voxels = 12 mm, exactly as `glassbox/extract/hooks.py` (`HOOKS["mid"]`). The encoder is class-agnostic. Bottleneck and decoder_F are not used.
- **Kidney arm:** label 133 + point label 1, one positive point at the lesion's interior point (max distance to the boundary), as W2 v3 (`glassbox/seg/w2_arms.py`, `scripts/w2v2_run.py`). **Main-set lesions only** (kidney 209 lesions, 48 bright, 161 dark, 201 cases); the 10 pilot lesions are excluded everywhere in W2-M. Bright = lesion mean HU minus kidney-parenchyma 5 to 20 mm shell mean > 0.
- **Patient-level:** every split, bootstrap and CV unit is the case (patient). Bootstrap = 1000 resamples of cases, seed 0, percentile 95% CIs. 5-fold GroupKFold by case (sorted case id, fold = index mod 5) wherever a kidney-native fit is needed.
- **Tokens (as the HCC voxel probe):** TUMOUR token = ground-truth `Mass` coverage >= 0.5 of the 12 mm cell; KID token = `Mass` coverage 0 and `Kidney` (label 1) coverage >= 0.5; tokens kept only if the token centre lies inside the GT kidney+mass dilated by 10 mm; "interior" tumour token = more than 2 mm from the nearest non-tumour GT voxel. Tumour tokens come only from main-set primary lesions (tokens in pilot lesions or in other `Mass` components are dropped, as are mixed tokens); bright/dark = the lesion's sign. Sliding windows overlap 0.3 and all copies are kept; a patient never straddles folds.
- **Probe spec (identical everywhere):** `StandardScaler` + L2 `LogisticRegression(C=0.1, class_weight="balanced", max_iter=2000)`, training sets capped at 200 tokens per (patient, group), seed 0; evaluation on all tokens. Controls: (i) randomly initialised network (`torch.manual_seed(0)`), same hooks and probes; (ii) raw-HU: 14 local HU features per token (mean, std, p5, p25, p50, p75, p95 inside the cell and over the 3x-cell cube).
- **HCC side:** the 89-case clean HCC cohort's stored tokens (`cache/vp/trained`, random-init tokens, `mid_hu14`), the same tokens the HCC results used. **Sanity check 1 (stop on failure):** re-running the existing HCC probe analysis code on the stored HCC tokens must reproduce `results/voxel_probe/probe_table.csv` exactly.
- **Determinism checks (M2, M3; stop on failure):** the unhooked kidney arm re-run on 10 fixed main-set lesions must reproduce the W2 v3 CSV (Dice within 2e-5); an identity hook (no erasure) must reproduce the unhooked outputs.
- **Save all predicted masks** (full volume, compressed, with spacing, affine, point) for every GPU run.

## M1. Cross-organ transfer of the tumour concept (GPU: kidney tokens; CPU: probes)
- **Extraction:** run the kidney volumes (the 201 main-set cases, GT labels from `results/w2_kidney`) through the same sliding-window pass as the HCC extraction (overlap 0.3, `RoiTokenRecorder`-style ROI tokens, automatic kidney prompts [5, 14] only to drive the pass; the encoder tokens do not depend on the prompt), for the trained network and for the random-init network. Raw-HU features come from the CT (CPU). An allowed speed-up, to be verified before use: running only the windows that intersect the kidney ROI must give token tables identical to the full grid on 3 cases.
- **Transfer probe:** the HCC probe A (tumour vs liver tokens) fitted ONCE on all 89 HCC cases' trained mid tokens with the spec above, applied UNCHANGED (weights and HCC scaler) to the kidney tokens (tumour vs kidney parenchyma). Transfer controls through the same pipeline: random-init (HCC random-init tokens train, kidney random-init tokens test) and raw-HU (HCC 14-feature probe applied to the kidney HU features).
- **Kidney-native ceiling:** the same probe spec trained on kidney tokens with 5-fold GroupKFold by case, with its own random-init and raw-HU controls.
- **Metrics (interior tumour tokens vs KID tokens):** AUROC (primary) and detection = share of tumour tokens above a threshold set at 95% specificity on the KID tokens of the training folds (a one-scalar recalibration of the threshold per fold, weights untouched; detection at the unchanged HCC-derived threshold is reported too). Reported overall and split bright vs dark (the bright-minus-dark AUROC difference with CI is descriptive), with the number of patients and lesions contributing tumour tokens and the number of lesions that have no tumour token at the mid resolution (12 mm cells).
- **Controls (fixed):** the HCC-trained random-init probe and the HCC-trained raw-HU probe, each transferred to kidney the same way as the trained probe (HCC tokens/features train, kidney tokens/features test, no refitting).
- **Decision rule (three tiers):** with AUC_T = transfer AUROC (trained network), AUC_N = kidney-native AUROC (trained network), and the CI = patient-level bootstrap 95% CI:
  - **SHARED** iff (1) the lower CI bound of AUC_T exceeds the upper CI bounds of BOTH transfer controls (random-init and raw-HU); AND (2) the lower CI bound of AUC_T exceeds 0.5; AND (3) (AUC_T - 0.5) / (AUC_N - 0.5) >= 0.70 (point estimate), i.e. transfer recovers at least 70% of the kidney-native above-chance signal.
  - **PARTIAL** iff (1) and (2) hold but (3) fails (fraction < 0.70).
  - **ORGAN-SPECIFIC** otherwise. If AUC_T is significantly BELOW 0.5 this is stated (the concept is reversed, not merely absent).
- **GPU time estimate:** the W2 point-window runs took about 8 s per lesion including preprocessing (3 to 8 s per case for loading and resampling). A whole-volume sliding-window pass with a recorder is several times a point-window pass; I estimate 20 to 40 s per case per network, so trained + random-init over 201 cases is roughly 2.2 to 4.5 h, or about 1 to 2 h with the window-skipping speed-up.
- **Timing and stop rule (approved):** time 3 cases first (trained and random-init) and project the full M1 extraction; **if the projection exceeds 3.5 h, stop and report** (a case cap would be an inclusion change and needs sign-off). **The window-skipping optimisation (running only the windows that intersect the kidney ROI) may be used only after a 3-case token identity check passes** (the token tables must equal the full-grid pass exactly on those 3 cases); otherwise the full grid is used.

## M2. Cross-organ erasure (GPU), with a branch fixed now
- **Branch (pre-specified, set by the M1 verdict):**
  - **M1 = SHARED or PARTIAL -> primary = HCC-fitted direction.** Rank-1 LEACE (closed form, covariance ridge 1e-4 x mean eigenvalue) for the concept "token is tumour vs liver", fitted ONCE on all 89 HCC cases' trained mid tokens (as `scripts/mech_b_fit.py`, but on all cases because no HCC data are evaluated), applied as the affine map `x - d (a.(x - mu)) / c` to the 192-channel stage-2 feature map in every window (the same hook as HCC B). Nothing about kidney data is used to fit it.
  - **M1 = ORGAN-SPECIFIC -> primary = kidney-native rank-1 LEACE** (concept: tumour vs kidney-parenchyma tokens from the M1 kidney extraction) fitted on the TRAINING folds (5-fold GroupKFold by case, as M1) and applied to the lesions of the held-out patients only; same controls and margins. **The HCC-direction erasure is run as a secondary arm either way.**
- **Controls at the same rank:** (a) a random unit direction erased by orthogonal projection through the training-token mean (seed = kidney case index; for the HCC-direction arm, the HCC token mean); (b) LEACE for the local-HU concept (cell mean HU) fitted on the same tokens as the primary eraser.
- **Runs:** the kidney arm (133 / point label 1) on the 209 main-set lesions under: LEACE, random, HU (primary-branch eraser), plus the HCC-direction LEACE as secondary if it is not the primary. The unhooked baseline is the W2 v3 run (verified by the determinism check).
- **Kidney organ check on a seeded 60-case subset:** kidney organ Dice (prediction vs GT `Kidney`+`Mass`, annotated slices) from the kidney organ classes 5 and 14 with a point at the interior of the kidney containing the lesion, unhooked and under each hook; the class (5 or 14) is selected per case on the UNHOOKED run (higher Dice) and then fixed. **Subset: the 201 main-set case ids sorted, then `numpy.random.default_rng(133).choice(201, 60, replace=False)`; the list is written to `results/w2_mech/m2_organ_subset.csv` before any M2 run.**
- **Outcome:** per lesion, change in `dice_full` (hooked minus unhooked) and in coverage; per case, change in kidney organ Dice (60-case subset). **Secondary: the change in the LEAK and UNDER rates under erasure (LEACE, random, HU), with case-level bootstrap CIs.**
- **Decision rule (proposed margins, approved):** **USED** iff (1) the mean kidney lesion Dice change under LEACE is <= -0.05 with a CI excluding zero; AND (2) the paired difference (LEACE minus random) and (LEACE minus HU) each has a CI whose upper bound is below -0.02; AND (3) the mean kidney organ Dice change under LEACE has a CI lower bound >= -0.05 (organ Dice holds; the HCC rule used 0.05). If (3) fails the verdict is reported as "erasure too blunt" in addition. **NOT NECESSARY** otherwise. A mean lesion Dice change under LEACE that is positive with a CI excluding zero is stated as "erasure improves".
- **GPU time estimate:** about 8 s per lesion-run, so three hooked arms x 209 lesions is about 1.4 h (four arms about 1.9 h if the secondary HCC-direction arm is needed), plus the organ check on 60 cases (about 5 runs per case at about 6 s, about 0.5 h). Total about 2 to 2.5 h.

## M3. Kidney contrast counterfactual (GPU)
- **Edit (same procedure as `results/contrast_cf`):** add a constant HU offset inside the lesion's GT mask only, weight 1 for interior voxels and 0.5 on the 1-voxel inner border (6-connected erosion), clip to [-1024, 3071], record the realised mean (flag, do not drop, if more than 5 HU from the target). T and L are the lesion and 5 to 20 mm kidney-parenchyma shell means (`tumour_hu`, `liver_hu` in `results/w2_kidney/gt_lesions.csv`), c = T - L. The prompt point and everything else are unchanged; edited volumes go through the unchanged preprocessing and the kidney arm.
- **Arm A (dark to bright):** main-set dark lesions whose unedited run is not LEAK. Edit: target mean L + |c| (sign flips, equal magnitude). Control A (sign-keeping): target T - 2|c|.
- **Arm B (bright to dark):** main-set bright lesions whose unedited run is LEAK. Edit: target L - |c|. Control B (sign-keeping): target T + 2|c|.
- **Primary outcome: change in LEAK rate.** LEAK = predicted/lesion volume >= 3x or inside-lesion share <= 0.3 (the W2 v3 definition). Per lesion D = LEAK(edit) - LEAK(control), paired; arm statistic = mean D with a case-level bootstrap CI.
- **Decision rule (proposed, approved):** **SUPPORTED** iff Arm A has CI lower > 0 and point >= 0.10 AND Arm B has CI upper < 0 and point <= -0.10. **PARTIAL** if exactly one arm meets its condition. **NOT SUPPORTED** if neither arm meets its condition and the point estimates are zero or in the wrong direction with at least 10 lesions per arm. **INCONCLUSIVE** otherwise (including an arm with fewer than 10 lesions).
- **Secondary:** edit-minus-control change in Dice and in coverage; failure-mode shifts.
- **Exploratory (labelled EXPLORATORY, as the HCC bridge):** paired projection drop of the mid-encoder tokens inside the lesion (token centre inside the lesion, as M1) onto (i) the HCC probe direction and (ii) the kidney-native direction, edited minus unedited within lesion, and its Spearman correlation with the Dice change, with patient-bootstrap CIs.
- **GPU time estimate:** about 135 Arm A lesions x 2 conditions plus about 19 Arm B lesions x 2 conditions is roughly 310 edited runs at about 15 s (edit, write, re-preprocess, infer) = about 1.3 h, plus about 0.3 h for the unedited token-recording runs: about 1.5 to 1.8 h.

## Named confounds (stated in advance, repeated in the write-up)
- **Possible in-distribution images:** C4KC-KiTS may have been seen in training via AbdomenCT-1K, with tumour labelling there unknown.
- **Arterial/corticomedullary phase:** bright renal cortex lies inside the parenchyma reference, so "kidney parenchyma" tokens and the contrast reference are heterogeneous.
- **Point prompts give location:** the arm measures segmentation quality given the lesion's location; the encoder tokens are computed without the point.
- Also: the kidney failure is mainly LEAK while the HCC concept evidence concerns missed tumour, so a shared concept need not map onto the kidney failure; the zero-shot route has no tumour class; tokens are 12 mm cells and small lesions have few or no tumour tokens (resolution loss); HU edits change the mean only, not texture, edges or enhancement pattern; the HCC cohort is a different organ, phase mix and tumour type; the W2 v3 kidney GAP point estimate is just above the margin; no multiplicity correction across M1, M2, M3.
