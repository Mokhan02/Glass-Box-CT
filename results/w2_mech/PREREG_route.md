# Pre-registration W2-M route check: is M2's null due to the zero-shot route rather than the organ?

Status: APPROVED (2026-10-18) with two edits (the decision rule below replaces the M2-style verdict for this question; a positive-control arm was added) and committed on `research/roadmap` BEFORE any run. Deviations go to `results/w2_mech/DEVIATIONS.md`. **Inclusion or exclusion changes need the researcher's sign-off before they are applied, even pre-analysis.** Nothing here touches `main`.

## Question
In the kidney arm (label 133 + point label 1), erasing the kidney-native tumour direction at the mid-encoder changed lesion Dice by only -0.015 [-0.028, -0.002] (M2, NOT NECESSARY), whereas in HCC mech B the same kind of erasure cost lesion Dice -0.33 [-0.38, -0.29] (`results/mech/B_SUMMARY.md`). Two things differ between those results: the organ and the route (mech B used the class-26 automatic sliding-window pass; M2 used the zero-shot point route 133/1). This check holds the route fixed and changes only the organ back to HCC: **if erasing the HCC direction on HCC lesions under label 133 + point label 1 is also nearly inert, the route explains M2; if it is clearly harmful, kidney differs from liver even on the same route.**

## Data and arms
- **Lesions:** the HCC main set of W2 v3: 76 lesions (23 bright, 53 dark) in 68 cases (the HCC-TACE-Seg UID-route cohort, two largest lesions >= 10 mm per case, minus the 10 v2 pilot lesions; every one of the 68 cases is inside the clean-89 cohort used by mech B). Bright = lesion mean HU minus liver 5 to 20 mm shell mean > 0. Pilot lesions excluded, as everywhere in W2.
- **Primary arm:** label 133 + point label 1, one positive point at the lesion's interior point (the W2 v3 arm `hcc_133_1`). Baseline = the W2 v3 unhooked run of that arm.
- **Positive control arm (added at approval):** class 26, AUTOMATIC route (no point), the same 76 lesions, the same cross-fitted erasers and the same random and HU controls. It is the route mech B used (the class-26 sliding-window pass), so it should reproduce a mech-B-sized drop; if it does not, the erasers or hook are not doing what they did in mech B and nothing can be read from the 133/1 result. Its unhooked baseline is run in this check (the W2 v3 arms have no automatic run). Differences from mech B, stated: mech B prompted classes [1, 26] together and restricted the readout to the predicted-liver ROI; this arm prompts class 26 alone over the whole volume (probability > 0.5 on the original grid), so it reproduces mech B's route, not its exact numbers.
- **Secondary arm:** class 26 + point label 3 (the supported tumour class with a click, the W2 v3 arm `hcc_26_3`); same erasers, same rule; baseline = the W2 v3 unhooked run of that arm.

## Erasers: the HCC mech-B directions, cross-fitted
- **Mech B was already cross-fitted** (`scripts/mech_b_fit.py`, `scripts/mech_b_run.py`): for each of 5 folds (fold = index of the case in the sorted clean-89 case list mod 5) the eraser is fitted on the TRAINING folds' trained mid tokens and applied only to the held-out fold. These HCC lesions were part of the data from which the OTHER folds' erasers were fitted, but each lesion is run under the eraser of its own fold, fitted on tokens that exclude its own patient. So no lesion is processed under an eraser fitted on its own patient; I use these stored cross-fitted erasers (not an all-case fit), and the in-sample direction the researcher flagged is not used.
- **Directions (as mech B):** (a) rank-1 LEACE for the concept "token is tumour vs liver" (closed form, covariance ridge 1e-4 x mean eigenvalue); (b) HU control: LEACE for the local-HU concept (cell mean HU, continuous); (c) random control: a random unit direction erased by orthogonal projection through the training-token mean (seed 100 + fold, as mech B). Same hook as M2 and mech B: output of `image_encoder.encoder.layers[2]["blocks"]` (192 channels), every window, affine map `x - d (a.(x - mu)) / c`.
- If the stored `erasers.npz` of mech B cannot be found on the persistent filesystem, it is re-fitted with the unchanged `scripts/mech_b_fit.py` on the stored HCC tokens (deterministic) and any mismatch with the stored HCC results is reported.

## Outcomes and checks
- **Positive-control statistic:** the change in lesion Dice within 10 mm of the lesion (the mech B definition) under LEACE, on the lesions the unhooked automatic run detects (coverage >= 10%, the mech B population; the all-76 change is also reported), with the same bootstrap; reported alongside mech B's -0.33 [-0.38, -0.29].
- **Per lesion:** change in `dice_full` (hooked minus unhooked; the W2 v3 definition, primary) under LEACE, random and HU, per arm; also reported: coverage, the failure-mode shifts (LEAK / UNDER / OK), and the lesion Dice within 10 mm of the lesion (the mech B definition) so the number can be set beside mech B's -0.33.
- **Determinism checks (stop on failure):** the unhooked HCC arms re-run on 10 fixed main-set lesions (sorted by (case, rank), indices 0, 8, 16, ...) must reproduce the W2 v3 CSV (dice_full and coverage within 2e-5, same point); an identity hook (d = 0) must give bit-identical predictions.
- **Liver organ check (guard against a blunt eraser):** liver Dice (prediction vs GT liver+tumour, annotated slices) from class 1 with one point at the interior of the liver containing the lesion (point label 1), unhooked and under each eraser, on a seeded subset of 30 of the 68 cases (sorted main-set HCC case ids, `numpy.random.default_rng(133).choice(68, 30, replace=False)`; the list is written to `results/w2_mech/route_organ_subset.csv` before the run).
- **Statistics:** case-level bootstrap, 1000 resamples, seed 0, percentile 95% CIs; patient-level throughout.

## Decision rule (replaces the M2-style verdict for this question; on the primary arm HCC 133/1)
Let dD = the mean change in `dice_full` (hooked minus unhooked) under HCC-direction LEACE on the primary arm with its case-level bootstrap CI, and PC = the positive-control statistic above.
- **ORGAN DIFFERS:** the M2 USED criteria are met on 133/1: (1) dD <= -0.05 with CI upper < 0; AND (2) the paired differences (LEACE minus random) and (LEACE minus HU) each have a CI upper bound < -0.02; AND (3) the liver organ Dice change under LEACE has a CI lower bound >= -0.05 (seeded 30-case subset).
- **ROUTE EXPLAINS M2:** the lower CI bound of dD is > -0.10 AND the positive control shows a large effect (PC CI upper bound < -0.10).
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** otherwise, including the positive control failing (PC CI upper >= -0.10), or erasure too blunt (condition 3 failing).
**Order (mutually exclusive; my addition for the overlap region):** ORGAN DIFFERS is evaluated first, ROUTE EXPLAINS only if ORGAN DIFFERS is not met, INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. The two conditions can both hold in a narrow band (for example dD about -0.06 with CI [-0.09, -0.03]); ORGAN DIFFERS takes precedence there.
**Reported as secondary:** the M2-style verdict on 133/1 (USED / NOT NECESSARY with the "too blunt" and "improves" flags), the same statistics for the secondary arm 26/3, and, descriptively, dD and PC beside M2's -0.015 [-0.028, -0.002] and mech B's -0.33 [-0.38, -0.29]. A 133/1 result that is nearly inert while 26/3 is USED would suggest the tumour-class route carries the dependence; both inert on the point routes would say the dependence seen in mech B does not appear on either point route, without separating the zero-shot route from the point-window pass.

## Power and limits, stated in advance
- **The route rule's two thresholds are my reading of the researcher's edit:** dD CI lower > -0.10 means "no large drop is compatible with the data", which needs a CI half-width small enough to exclude -0.10; PC CI upper < -0.10 means the positive control is clearly large.
- **Power is lower than in M2:** 76 lesions in 68 cases (23 bright) vs 209 in 201 for M2; the CI half-width of the Dice change is likely about 0.03 or more (M2: about 0.013), so condition (2) (upper bound < -0.02) is hard to meet and a NOT NECESSARY verdict can partly reflect low power. The point estimate and CI are therefore reported beside both references and the verdict is read with that in mind.
- The hook acts on every window of the point-window pass; mech B acted on every window of the sliding-window pass; the encoder is class-agnostic but the windows seen differ.
- HCC lesions differ from kidney lesions in tumour type, contrast phase mix (HCC-TACE-Seg mixes acquisitions and its phase cannot be labelled) and size; this check separates route from organ only to the extent the HCC lesions behave like the mech B lesions.
- Dice change is a lesion-level mean over lesions that the unhooked arm already segments to varying degrees; HCC under 133/1 has a v3 Dice gap and a different baseline Dice from the mech B population.
- C4KC-KiTS confounds do not apply to HCC; the HCC data are out of distribution for training as far as known (HCC-TACE-Seg was not in the training table).
- No multiplicity correction; the secondary arm is descriptive for the reading above.

## GPU time estimate
About 6.5 s per point lesion-run: 76 lesions x 2 arms x 3 erasers = 456 runs, about 50 min; HCC preprocessing 68 cases at about 6 s, about 7 min; liver organ check 30 cases x (1 unhooked + 3 hooked) x about 6 s, about 12 min; **positive control: one whole-volume sliding-window pass per case and condition, 68 cases x 4 conditions (unhooked, LEACE, random, HU) = 272 passes at an estimated 10 to 25 s, about 45 to 115 min.** **Total about 2 to 3.2 h** on one A10. **Timing and stop rule:** the run first does the 10-lesion determinism check and times 3 cases with every condition; if the projection exceeds 3.5 h it stops and reports (a lesion cap would be an inclusion change needing sign-off).
