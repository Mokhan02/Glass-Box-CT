## 1. Sanity check 1 did not reproduce exactly (2026-10-18); disclosed, extraction continued, M1 verdict held pending the researcher's decision
PREREG check 1 requires re-running the existing HCC probe analysis (`scripts/voxel_probe_analyze.py`) on the stored HCC token tables to reproduce `results/voxel_probe/probe_table.csv` exactly, and says to stop on failure. Result (`hcc_repro_probe_table.csv` vs the stored table): 1086 numeric cells, 228 differ, maximum difference 0.007; 16 cells differ by more than 0.001 and 2 by more than 0.005. **The primary HCC row reproduces exactly** (trained, mid, activations, probe A, pooled: interior-FN detection 0.5873 [0.5513, 0.656], liver false-positive rate 0.0533); trained mid-activation rows differ in 5 cells by at most 0.0001. By layer, maximum differences: mid 0.0023, bottleneck 0.0070 (small-tumour subset, 768-d), decoder_F 0.0010; random-init rows at most 0.0004. The pattern (larger differences in higher-dimensional layers and small subsets) suggests numerical differences in the logistic-regression solver between library versions (this environment: scikit-learn 1.7.2, numpy 2.2.6), but that explanation is not tested. The stored table was produced in an earlier environment that no longer exists.
Consequence: the rule "reproduce exactly" is not met. The kidney token extraction (GPU) is independent of this check and was left running; the M1 analysis and verdict are held for the researcher's decision on whether the near-exact reproduction is acceptable. Any M1 numbers will be reported with this deviation attached. Nothing about M1's rules or thresholds was changed.
