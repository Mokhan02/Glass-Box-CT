## 18. W4: the registration is not bit-reproducible; the driver's equality check was too strict (2026-11-02, 20:16 EST; before any W4 prediction, no detection or analysis outcome read)
The W4 driver (`scripts/w4_waw_run_instance.sh`) rebuilds the ground truth of each half of the patients (`scripts/w4_waw_paired_contrast.py --save-dir`: same registration seed 1, 2 ITK threads per worker, 12 workers) and compared the resulting tumour table with the committed host1 cohort table, failing on any difference above 0.01 HU. Both halves stopped on that check (half 0 at 20:16 EST on host1, half 1 at 20:17 EST on the second instance) before any GPU step. **Cause:** the multithreaded ITK registration (Mattes MI, sampled metric, threaded reductions) is not bit-reproducible across runs or machines; the drawn-phase contrasts (the mask itself) are identical (0.00 HU), the propagated-phase contrasts are not. This is consistent with the Mac-versus-host1 cross-check of the scouting (max 1.8 HU on a 15-patient sample, no category or QA change); I had set a tolerance (0.01 HU) tighter than that already-measured reproducibility, which is my error, not an infrastructure failure.
