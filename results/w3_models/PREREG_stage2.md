## Question and gate
Stage 1 found a bright-versus-dark gap in model A (CLIP-Driven Universal Model, Swin UNETR) and model D (nnU-Net v2, Task03, fold 0): both "GAP (detection and Dice)" (`results/w3_models/stage1/STAGE1_SUMMARY.md`), so both pass the Stage 2 gate of PREREG_stage1. In VISTA-3D the mechanism was: the mid-encoder represents the missed tumour tissue but the loss happens late (`results/voxel_probe`), erasing the tumour-vs-liver direction at the mid-encoder costs lesion Dice (`results/mech` B, -0.33), and brightening dark tumours causes misses (`results/contrast_cf`). **Does the same mechanism appear in A and D?**

The W3 primary after Amendment 3: HCC UID route, **74 cases / 85 lesions (28 bright, 57 dark)**, HCC_019_773068 scored against its corrected label (Amendment 1), HCC_027_967743 excluded as a source-label error and added back in the sensitivity (it is run too, so the including-analysis is available). **Choice and why (CONFIRMED by the researcher):** stride 8 and 12 mm tokens cannot coincide for D because nnU-Net resamples to 0.77 mm, not 1.5 mm. Stage 3 matches VISTA's procedure exactly (a native token of about 6 mm in-plane, probed after a 2x average pool to 12 mm, intervened on at native resolution, which commutes with the pooling) and matches model A's stride 8 (a quarter of the maximum stride 32, the same relative depth). Stage 4 matches the 12 mm size natively but sits at half the maximum stride. **Stage 3 is the primary hook for M1, M2 and M3; stage 4 is recorded in the same pass and reported as a probe-only hook sensitivity (M1).** **Limitation (stated): the through-plane token is 16 mm (anisotropic: 12.2 x 12.2 x 16 mm after the pool), unlike VISTA's isotropic 12 mm.** **Real-case verification is the first step of the first GPU session (see below); the architecture-level shape check is `results/w3_models/stage2/hook_check_D_architecture.json`.**
- **"Ladder" for the probe (mirrors VISTA `mid` / `bottleneck` / `decoder_F`):** D: mid = stage 3 (pooled x2), bottleneck = stage 5 (reported only), decoder_F = the last decoder stage output before the segmentation head (32 channels, pooled x16 = 12.3 mm in-plane). A: mid = `swinViT.layers2[0]` output (hidden state 2 = sensitivity), bottleneck = hidden state 4 (768 channels, 48 mm, reported only), decoder_F = the 48-channel decoder output before `precls_conv` (pooled x8 = 12 mm); each shape is checked on a real case in the first-session hook check.
- **Probe tensor and intervention site (CONFIRMED): the same tensor.** A: the M1 primary probe is fitted on, and the M2 / M3 eraser and edit act on, the output of `backbone.swinViT.layers2[0]` (192 channels, stride 8: the feature stream into `layers3`, which also feeds the skip copy through `proj_out`'s LayerNorm), so LEACE is fitted on the tensor it edits. **The hidden-state-2 probe (the LayerNorm copy that Gate 0 verified) is a sensitivity row for M1.** D: forward hook on `encoder.stages[3]` replacing its output (the next stage and the skip connection both see it); the probe tensor is that same stage-3 output (pooled x2 for the probe, native for the intervention). **Verification before any experiment, on real cases with the trained checkpoints (`scripts/w3s2_hook_check.py`): an edit at the intervention tensor must reach BOTH consumers: A: hidden state 2 equals LayerNorm(edited `layers2[0]` output) AND hidden states 3 and 4 and the logits change; D: the encoder's returned skip 3 equals the edited stage-3 output exactly AND skip 4, the first decoder stage and the logits change.**

## M1. Probe ladder: is the missed tumour represented at the hook, and lost late? (GPU: token extraction; CPU: probes)
The voxel probe of `results/voxel_probe/PREREG.md`, unchanged in its definitions and thresholds, run per model on the Stage 2 cohort.
- **Tokens:** from every window of the model's own sliding-window pass (A: overlap 0.5; D: step 0.5, one non-mirrored forward pass per window for the recorder, while the prediction used for TP/FN is the model's default output), all overlap copies kept. TUMOUR token: GT tumour coverage >= 0.5 of the token cell; LIV token: GT tumour coverage 0 and GT liver (non-tumour) coverage >= 0.5; other tokens dropped; kept only if the token centre lies inside GT liver + tumour dilated by 10 mm; annotated slices only (tokens with < 50% annotated voxels dropped); TP = tumour token with predicted-tumour coverage >= 0.5, FN = below; interior = more than 2 mm from the nearest non-tumour GT voxel (GT spacing). The cell is the native token cell of each model at the hook (pooled as in the table above).
- **Probe spec (not tuned):** 5-fold GroupKFold by patient (fixed order), `StandardScaler` + L2 `LogisticRegression(C=0.1, class_weight="balanced", max_iter=2000)`, training sets capped at 200 tokens per (patient, group), seed 0, evaluation on all held-out tokens; Probe A = TUMOUR vs LIV; Probe B = TP vs LIV evaluated on FN; decision threshold = 95th percentile of LIV scores of the training folds.
- **Controls (same tokens):** (i) local raw-HU: the 14 features of the VISTA PREREG (7 statistics inside the token cell and over the 3x-cell cube; HU from the CT); (ii) randomly initialised copy of the same network (`torch.manual_seed(0)`), same hooks and probes; (iii) strata small vs large tumours (median split by GT tumour volume).
- **Head check:** the median "tumour margin" of interior-FN voxels (GT tumour voxels on annotated slices, > 2 mm from the GT edge, predicted not tumour): A = the class 27 logit; D = z_tumour minus max(z_liver, z_background) (the softmax network has no single tumour logit); median, IQR and the share within one unit below 0. Threshold recovery is NOT re-run (not part of the mechanism claim).
- **Verdict rule per model (the VISTA rule, fixed; the four categories, all spelled out):** R_enc = Probe A detection of interior-FN tokens at `mid`, CI_enc its CI, R_hu the raw-HU control's rate with CI_hu. **REPRESENTED**: R_enc >= 0.50 AND CI_enc lower > CI_hu upper. **LOST LATE**: REPRESENTED AND at least one of (a) decoder_F Probe A detection lower than R_enc by >= 0.15 with a patient-bootstrap CI of the difference excluding 0, or (b) median tumour margin of interior-FN voxels <= -2.0 (near miss: > -2.0). **NEVER REPRESENTED**: CI_enc lower <= CI_hu upper. Anything else, for example above the HU control but R_enc < 0.50: **INCONCLUSIVE**. (REPRESENTED here means the encoder represents the missed tissue but the pre-registered 'lost late' criteria are not met; LOST LATE implies REPRESENTED.) Interpretation note (unchanged): if the random-init rate's CI overlaps the trained model's, "represents" is qualified as "separable in features a random network also provides". Bottleneck reported, not used.
- **Hook sensitivity (probe-only, same rule, reported beside the primary, not part of the cross-model reading):** D stage 4; A hidden state 2. **Model A U-Net sensitivity arm (deferred from Stage 1):** the CLIP-Driven U-Net checkpoint (`clip_driven_universal_unet.pth`, 19.4 M parameters) through its shipped pipeline on the same cohort; hook = `backbone.down_tr256` output 0 (256 channels, stride 8 = 12 mm at 1.5 mm; bottleneck = `down_tr512` output 0, 512 channels; decoder_F = `backbone.up_tr64`, 64 channels pooled x8), shapes verified on a real case in the hook check; M1 only, same rule, reported separately, **not part of the cross-model reading**.
- **Sanity check before any new extraction (stop on failure, CPU):** the probe analysis code (shared with the VISTA work) run on the stored VISTA tokens must reproduce `results/voxel_probe/probe_table.csv` exactly.
- **GPU time estimate:** A trained about 25 s per case x 75 = 0.5 h, random-init 0.5 h, U-Net sensitivity about 10 s per case = 0.2 h; D trained and random-init about 8 s per non-mirrored pass x 75 x 2 = 0.35 h; plus the default-prediction passes already saved in Stage 1. D extraction also runs the model's default (mirrored) prediction on the preprocessed grid, about 45 s per case, plus the recorder passes: D about 1.2 h, A about 1.2 h (prediction about 20 s + recorder passes for the trained and random-init networks about 30 s per case), U-Net sensitivity about 0.3 h. **About 2.7 h in total (revised).**

## M2. Cross-fitted rank-1 LEACE at the hook: necessity (GPU)
Mirrors `results/mech` B (same margins), with the cross-fitting of `results/w2_mech`.
- **Eraser:** rank-1 LEACE (closed form, covariance ridge 1e-4 x mean eigenvalue) for the concept "token is tumour vs liver", fitted on the TRAINING folds' trained mid tokens (the M1 token tables, tokens of the primary cohort only) and applied as the affine map `x - d (a.(x - mu)) / c` to the feature map at the intervention site in every window (it commutes with the average pooling used for tokens). Each case is run only under the eraser of its own fold, fitted on tokens that exclude its patient.
- **Controls at the same rank:** (a) a random unit direction erased by orthogonal projection through the training-token mean (seed 100 + fold); (b) LEACE for the local-HU concept (cell mean HU, continuous) on the same tokens.
- **Population and outcome (mech B):** lesions the UNHOOKED model detects (coverage >= 0.10); per lesion the change in lesion Dice and in coverage, and whether it is still detected; **liver guard:** per-case liver Dice (prediction vs GT liver = labels 1 and 2, annotated slices) before and after, on every primary case (one whole-volume pass per condition gives tumour and liver together).
- **Probe check (mech B):** a probe trained on erased training-fold tokens and tested on erased held-out tokens must be near chance (held-out AUROC upper CI <= 0.60; the same probe before erasure is reported); if not, the run is reported as not testing necessity.
- **Verdict (mech B margins, fixed; all categories):** **USED** iff the mean change in lesion Dice under LEACE is below zero with a CI excluding zero AND the paired differences (LEACE minus random) and (LEACE minus HU) each have a CI entirely below zero. **NOT NECESSARY** otherwise. If the mean liver Dice drops by more than 0.05 under LEACE it is also reported as **"erasure too blunt"**, and **a "too blunt" or "not testing necessity" run does not count as "meets M2"** in the cross-model reading. A positive mean change with a CI excluding zero is stated as "erasure improves". The categories are therefore: USED; USED + erasure too blunt; NOT NECESSARY; NOT NECESSARY + erasure improves; and either of these flagged "not testing necessity" if the post-erasure probe is not near chance. Descriptive context: the VISTA-3D effect was -0.33 [-0.38, -0.29] with random -0.002 and HU -0.045.
- **Runs:** per model, 4 conditions per case in one preprocessing: **base** (unhooked; the same Stage 1 pipeline, re-run in the session so it also saves the M4 decision-margin maps; it must reproduce the Stage 1 lesion coverage within 1e-3 and the same detection flags on EVERY case, a stop condition), **leace, random, hu** (the cross-fitted erasers); plus an **identity** hook (d = 0) on the first 3 cases, which must equal base in the same session. D under its default inference with mirroring ON, matching Stage 1 (decision of the researcher; the hook acts in every flipped pass). The runner is `scripts/w3s2_m2_run.py` (erasers fitted by `scripts/w3s2_m2_fit.py`, analysis `scripts/w3s2_m2_analyze.py`).
- **GPU time estimate (revised):** per case one pass per condition: A about 20 s per pass x 4 = 1.7 h for 75 cases, plus the base-map pass overhead, about **2.0 h**; D about 55 s per mirrored pass x 4 plus about 40 s CPU preprocessing per case = **about 4.6 h** (GPU-bound, so extra shards do not shorten it). **About 6.6 h for M2 including the M4 maps** (2.0 h + 4.6 h); if the researcher prefers, the M4 maps can be a separate pass later (A 0.4 h, D 1.3 h) and M2 shrinks to A 1.7 h + D 3.4 h.

## M4. Readout-fix generality: does a threshold shift fix A and D as it fixed VISTA-3D? (maps saved in the M2 baseline pass; CPU analysis)
**Question.** For VISTA-3D a single threshold shift on the tumour logit, chosen by nested cross-validation on patients, removed most of the one-sided sum-of-diameters bias (default -19.2% to -0.1% [-6.3, +2.7]) at the price of over-estimating >= 20% in 12.4% [5.6, 20.2] of cases (`results/trust`, `results/SUMMARY.md` finding 11). Does the same fix reduce the bright-versus-dark detection gap and the bias in models A and D, and at what over-estimation cost?
**Maps.** Stage 1 did NOT save probability maps or logits (only binary masks), so none exist for A or D. They are saved during the M2 baseline pass (unhooked, all 75 cases): the tumour **decision margin** on the model's own preprocessed grid, float16, annotated slab only (the VISTA `rr` cache analogue): A = the class-27 logit before the sigmoid (the shipped post-processing was inert in Stage 1, so the threshold is applied to the raw margin), D = z_tumour minus max(z_liver, z_background) (its sign is nnU-Net's argmax decision for tumour; margin 0 is the default). The GT label map on the same grid is saved with each map. **Stated limitation:** diameters and Dice are measured on the model grid (A 1.5 mm; D 1.0 x 0.77 x 0.77 mm), as in the VISTA recalibration analysis, not on the original grid; the default policy is recomputed on the same grid, so every comparison is paired.
**Policies and selection (exactly the VISTA nested design applied to the always-shifted policy).** Default: predicted tumour = margin > 0. Shifted: margin > t, t in {-0.5, -1.0, ..., -4.0}. Outer 5-fold CV by patient (fold = index in the sorted primary case list mod 5); inside each outer training set t is the grid value that minimises the mean absolute relative error of the sum of target diameters (an inner 4-fold CV over a one-parameter policy with nothing to fit equals the training-set mean, so it is computed that way; ties to the less negative t); the chosen t is applied to the held-out fold only. Lesions as in the hackathon RECIST analysis (26-connected tumour components on annotated slices, longest axial Feret diameter >= 10 mm, the two largest per case; detected = >= 10% of the lesion volume covered; predicted diameter = the largest overlapping predicted component, 0 for a miss). Bright / dark per lesion = the cohort lesion table (HCC_019 corrected), grid lesions matched to table ranks by diameter (mismatches above 25% are reported).
**Primary outcomes (per model, with case-level bootstrap 95% CIs, per-arm seed):** (1) the change in the bright-versus-dark detection gap (dark minus bright detection rate), shifted minus default; (2) the change in the absolute median signed sum-of-diameters error (the bias), shifted minus default. **Reported with them (the over-estimation cost):** the share of cases over-estimated by >= 20% under each policy and its paired change, the mean absolute error, the share with |error| >= 20%, the mean tumour Dice, detection in dark and in bright lesions, and the chosen threshold per fold.
**Verdict per model (proposed margins; for the researcher's confirmation before any run):** **FIX GENERALISES** iff (1) the gap change has a CI entirely below 0 and a point estimate <= -0.05 AND (2) the change in |bias| has a CI entirely below 0 and a point estimate <= -0.05. **PARTIAL** if exactly one of the two holds. **DOES NOT GENERALISE** if neither. Flagged **"with over-estimation cost"** if the share over-estimated by >= 20% rises by >= 0.10 or reaches >= 0.15 under the shift (VISTA reference 12.4%).
**Optional readout re-fit: NOT run.** It needs decoder voxel features (a separate extraction pass, an estimated 1 to 2 h of GPU, then the re-fit of the VISTA re-fit PREREG) and is done only if the researcher asks; the threshold shift is the primary M4.
**Place in the reading.** M4 is reported per model beside the VISTA numbers; it is not part of the "mechanism replicates" criterion (it asks whether a cheap fix transfers, not whether the mechanism does). Code: `glassbox/seg/w3s2_m4.py`, `scripts/w3s2_m4_analyze.py`, tested on synthetic maps (`tests/test_w3s2_m4.py`).

## M3. Brightness counterfactual (GPU), with the paired projection exploratory
Mirrors `results/contrast_cf` and its bridge. Each lesion is edited alone in its own copy of the volume; the outcome is measured on that lesion only; prediction = the model's primary pipeline.
- **Measurements:** L = mean HU of the non-tumour GT liver in the 5 to 20 mm shell, T = lesion mean HU, c = T - L (recomputed with the shared contrast function on the corrected label for HCC_019; baseline coverage and Dice recomputed in this experiment, once per case, not taken from Stage 1).
- **Arm A (dark to bright):** baseline detected (coverage >= 0.10) and c < 0; edit sets the lesion mean to L + |c|; **control A (sign-keeping)** sets it to T - 2|c|. **Arm B (reverse, bright to dark):** baseline coverage < 0.10 and c > 0; edit sets it to L - |c|; **control B** sets it to T + 2|c|. Edit procedure of `contrast_cf`: constant HU offset inside the GT mask only, weight 1 for interior voxels and 0.5 on the 1-voxel inner border, clip to [-1024, 3071], realised mean recorded (flagged, not dropped, if more than 5 HU from target); edited volumes written to scratch, deleted after use; the originals are never overwritten.
- **Primary outcome: change in coverage** (detection-level, not Dice). Arm A statistic D_A = delta_cov(edit) - delta_cov(control A) (expected < 0); Arm B statistic D_B = delta_cov(edit) - delta_cov(control B) (expected > 0); CIs on the median of delta_cov(edit) and of D, case-level bootstrap; paired exact two-sided sign test per arm. Secondary: lesion Dice with the same statistics.
- **Verdict (contrast_cf, fixed):** **SUPPORTED** only if (1) Arm A: median delta_cov(edit) < 0 with CI entirely below 0 and median D_A < 0 with CI entirely below 0; (2) Arm B: median delta_cov(edit) > 0 with CI entirely above 0 and median D_B > 0 with CI entirely above 0; (3) Arm A sign test p < 0.05 in the hypothesised direction; (4) Arm B likewise. **NOT SUPPORTED:** neither arm meets (1)/(2) and both median D point estimates are zero or in the wrong direction, with at least 10 lesions per arm. **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else, including an arm with fewer than 10 lesions. Four overlay figures per model chosen by the contrast_cf rule.
- **Exploratory (labelled EXPLORATORY, as the VISTA bridge):** for Arm A lesions, the paired projection drop (edited minus unedited, tokens whose centre lies inside the lesion, hook tokens as in M1) onto the model's OWN cross-fitted probe direction (w/sigma, unit-normalised; the held-out fold's training-fitted probe), and its Spearman correlation with the coverage drop, patient-bootstrap CIs. Not part of any verdict.
- **Expected sizes (from Stage 1):** Arm A about 48 (A) and 49 (D) dark detected lesions, Arm B about 21 (A) and 22 (D) bright missed lesions, so the 10-lesion minimum is met.
- **GPU time estimate:** baseline passes 75 + about 69 lesions x 2 conditions = about 213 whole-volume passes per model: A 213 x 25 s = 1.5 h; D 213 x 45 s = 2.7 h; **about 4.2 h in total** (A 1.5 h, D 2.7 h), plus the token recording (small, same passes).

## Overall cross-model reading (fixed now)
Per model the experiments give: M1 in {LOST LATE, REPRESENTED, NEVER REPRESENTED, INCONCLUSIVE}; M2 in {USED, USED + too blunt, NOT NECESSARY, NOT NECESSARY + erasure improves, each possibly + not testing necessity}; M3 in {SUPPORTED, NOT SUPPORTED, INCONCLUSIVE}.
- **"Meets M1" = LOST LATE** (the VISTA-3D M1 verdict). **"Meets M2" = USED, not too blunt, probe check passed.**
- **MECHANISM REPLICATES:** BOTH model A and model D meet M1 AND M2.
- **PARTIAL REPLICATION:** not the above, but both models reach at least REPRESENTED at M1 (REPRESENTED counts as partial replication, not as meeting M1), or one model meets M1 and M2 and the other is partial; the per-model verdicts are stated.
- **MECHANISM DOES NOT REPLICATE IN THIS FORM:** anything else (for example NEVER REPRESENTED or INCONCLUSIVE at M1 in either model, or NOT NECESSARY in both).
M3 is reported per model beside this reading and is not part of the criterion (it asks a different, causal-in-the-image question). The model A U-Net arm and the hook sensitivities are reported but never enter the reading. Models are never pooled.

## Named confounds and limits (stated in advance)
Hook choice for D (anisotropic 16 mm through-plane token; stage 3 vs 4) and for A (intervention on the pre-norm stream, not the skip copy); different decoders, so "lost late" is defined by each model's own decoder_F and head margin and is not strictly comparable to VISTA's; D's recorder uses a non-mirrored pass while its prediction uses mirroring; a rank-1 linear erasure at one layer tests one linear direction only, and a null would not show the concept is unused; random-init networks provide features that can separate tumour from liver (hence the control); HU edits change mean contrast only, not texture or enhancement pattern; HCC-TACE-Seg mixes acquisitions and phases (phase cannot be labelled); model A's training data may contain similar cases; single seed and single fold for model D; the small cohort (74 cases) gives wide CIs, especially for bright-lesion strata; no multiplicity correction across experiments and models; the quality-flagged cases (poor organ segmentation) stay in the primary.
