## (b) Sign of the contrast within the same tumour: conditional logistic regression (as pre-registered)
Sign OR (sign = contrast > 0 HU on that series; with phase, |contrast|, is_propagated, log diameter; patient-clustered bootstrap CI): **VISTA-3D 0.38 [0.01, 2.75] INCONCLUSIVE; D0 0.10 [0.02, 1.07] INCONCLUSIVE; Universal Model 0.15 [0.01, 0.48] OPPOSITE EFFECT; BRIGHT-50 11.2 [1.29, 2.5e5] REAL-PHASE BRIGHTNESS EFFECT** (rule: CI entirely above 1 / below 1 / otherwise). Discordant tumours 67 / 74 / 85 / 72 of 297 (sign-flippers among them: A-bright/P-dark 29 / 40 / 39 / 17, A-dark/P-bright 1 each). Phase OR 0.07 / 0.16 / 0.14 / 0.67 (arterial series detected less), is_propagated OR 0.24 / 0.88 / 0.49 / 0.36.
**Caution on the direction (my error in the PREREG, found only now).** The PREREG states prediction 3 and the verdict labels with the direction "detection HIGHER on the phase where the tumour is brighter (OR > 1)", which contradicts prediction 1 and W3, where bright tumours are detected LESS. The data follow prediction 1: in the tumours that are bright on one phase and dark on the other, detection is lower on the bright phase for VISTA-3D, D0 and the Universal Model (OR below 1; v1 effective-N design, 21 tumours, bright minus dark detection: VISTA-3D -0.29 [-0.53, -0.05], D0 -0.52 [-0.74, -0.30], Universal Model -0.57 [-0.79, -0.36], BRIGHT-50 +0.05 [-0.11, +0.22]; the v1 labels, which assume a positive effect, read NO SIGN EFFECT / INCONCLUSIVE). So under the labels as pre-registered the predicted effect is NOT found (and for the Universal Model the label reads OPPOSITE EFFECT), but the labels point the wrong way for the hypothesis they were meant to test; **reading the same estimates in the W3 direction (bright detected less) is a post-hoc interpretation, not a pre-registered verdict.** Only the Universal Model's CI excludes 1 in this direction; D0's upper bound is 1.07 (Wald 1.08).
**The conditional-logit estimates are fragile:** 1500 to 2000 of the 2000 bootstrap replicates needed the Jeffreys fit (about 70 discordant tumours, five covariates), the log-diameter coefficient is unidentifiable in practice (differences between a drawn and a propagated mask are tiny), the VISTA-3D phase and propagation ORs are near zero (near-separation), and verdicts change with the specification: without log diameter D0 / Universal Model / BRIGHT-50 keep their labels; without |contrast| the Universal Model stays OPPOSITE and BRIGHT-50 becomes INCONCLUSIVE; with the signed contrast D0 becomes OPPOSITE and the Universal Model INCONCLUSIVE; with a 5 HU dead zone only D0 reads OPPOSITE. BRIGHT-50's sign OR is not stable: it becomes INCONCLUSIVE with the 2 mm dilation, top-2 per series, the alignment-gate exclusion and the liver erosion. **The one comparatively robust within-tumour result is the descriptive v1 contrast above (bright phase detected 0.3 to 0.6 less than the dark phase in VISTA-3D, D0 and the Universal Model, not in BRIGHT-50).**
Power note (PREREG): the design had about 60% power for an OR of 3 in the pre-registered direction; INCONCLUSIVE is not evidence of absence.
