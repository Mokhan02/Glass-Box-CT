## Why they fail: stray predicted components, not misalignment
Every nnU-Net failure fails the **centroid-offset** criterion only (all 28 D0 and 26 B50; plus the flip flag in 540_1). The predicted liver is in the right place: the centroid of its **largest connected component** is within a median of about 1 to 2 mm of the GT organ centroid (D0 median offset xyz (1.0, -0.4, 1.1) mm; B50 (1.9, 1.4, 3.0) mm), while the centroid of the whole predicted organ is a median 21 mm (D0) and 26 mm (B50) away, mostly along x. The reason is that the nnU-Net output (default `nnUNetv2_predict`, no post-processing) contains additional predicted-liver components far from the liver: every failing series has more than one component, and in 22 of 28 (D0) and 21 of 26 (B50) the largest component holds less than 95% of the predicted volume; the centroid of the predicted voxels outside the GT organ lies a median 17 cm away (D0 (171, -48, -23) mm, B50 (167, -40, -20) mm). Overlays (`overlays/`): in 66_1 D0 and B50 label the **spleen** as liver (and B50 also under-segments the right lobe); in 33_2 both nnU-Net models segment only the central part of a heterogeneous liver (VISTA-3D and the Universal Model are accurate there); 146_1 was not inspected visually. The ground-truth organ is a TotalSegmentator liver, and VISTA-3D and the Universal Model agree with it in these series.
**540_1 (one series, both nnU-Net models; the only series with orientation code LSP, all others LAS):** the nnU-Net predictions are nearly empty (D0 177 mL, B50 376 mL against a GT organ of 1796 mL; Dice 0.08 and 0.13) while VISTA-3D and the Universal Model are accurate (0.979, 0.976). The files carry the input geometry (equal shape, affine, spacing), so this is not a writing or resampling error of the pipeline; nnU-Net v2 reads the array axes as given and the model was trained on LAS-like volumes, so a volume whose array axes are (L, S, P) is out of distribution. It is classified MODEL-SIDE by the gate's definition (no translation helps); I flag it as an input-orientation sensitivity of the nnU-Net models that the other two models (which reorient the volume on load) do not have. It is one series; its tumours are scored as they are, and the sensitivity excluding gate failures removes it.

## Clustering (D0 and B50 failures; no association found)
- **Same series fail in both nnU-Net models:** 21 series fail in both, 7 in D0 only, 5 in B50 only (Jaccard 0.64). 3 of the 4 VISTA-3D failures and 3 of the 6 Universal Model failures are in the nnU-Net failing set: the failures are partly series-specific (the ground truth or the anatomy), partly nnU-Net-specific (stray components).
- **By patient:** the 28 D0 failures are in 20 patients; 8 patients fail on both phases. The failures are patient-related, not phase-related.
- **By phase:** D0 11 of 98 arterial against 17 of 98 portal-venous series; B50 14 against 12 (no consistent direction).
- **By slice thickness / spacing / size / orientation:** fail rates by thickness are similar (2.5 mm: 15 of 78; 1.25 mm: 8 of 58; 5 mm: 2 of 11; 1.2 mm: 3 of 5); in-plane spacing, number of slices, GT organ volume, GT tumour volume and tumour share of the organ do not differ between failing and passing series (Mann-Whitney p 0.3 to 0.6 for the nnU-Net failing set against the rest); orientation: 27 of 28 D0 failures are LAS (195 of 196 series are LAS), the single LSP series fails (540_1). The WAW-TACE release metadata holds only slice thickness (no scanner field), so the scanner could not be checked.

## What this means for the analysis
No pipeline misalignment was found, so the analysis is not blocked. The gate failures are mostly extra predicted-liver components of the nnU-Net models (which do not change a tumour-level coverage measured inside the GT-labelled tumour) plus under-segmentation of a few livers. They enter as the pre-specified sensitivity (excluding series that fail the gate). **Half 0 (200 series): to be run the same way when its BRIGHT-50 predictions are scored; the conclusion above is for half 1 only.**
