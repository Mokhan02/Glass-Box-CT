# W4 scouting: WAW-TACE (Zenodo 12741586), go / no-go report

Branch `research/roadmap`. CPU only, no GPU used, no PREREG yet, **no model has been run on WAW-TACE and no detection outcome exists**. Research use only. Written 2026-11-02 (America/New_York). Rules fixed before the cohort run: `CONTRAST_RULES.md` (commits 075d3b5, 10f3989, dedfb46). Everything below is reproducible from the scripts and hashes listed in section 11.

## 0. Recommendation: GO, scoped (a) and (c) as the confirmatory pair, (b) as a pre-registered secondary with its power stated

| analysis | what the data allow | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| (a) second-cohort replication of the bright-versus-dark gap | 297 kept tumours (181 patients) with 242 bright and 55 dark on the arterial phase, 167 bright and 130 dark on the portal-venous phase (HCC-TACE-Seg had 28 bright and 57 dark); not in any model's training data on the published record (VISTA-3D caveat below) | **GO** |
| (c) D0 against BRIGHT-50 (and BRIGHT-25) on real arterial tumours | 202 kept tumours drawn on the arterial phase, 173 of them bright there; the arterial phase is the one where the failure should bite and where the synthetic augmentation was meant to help | **GO** |
| (b) paired within-tumour arterial versus portal-venous detection, sign separated from magnitude | effective N = **20 tumours in 18 patients** under the committed rule (delta 5 HU, rho 0.5); 8 to 44 across the grid; 85 tumours flip sign at all (delta 0), 41 beyond a 5 HU dead zone | **GO as a secondary, with the power stated in advance**: adequate for a large sign effect only (section 9) |

What would turn this into NO-GO for (b): if the researcher requires the sign-versus-magnitude test to be adequately powered for a 30-point effect, 20 tumours do not suffice (power about 0.36); a design without the magnitude matching (41 or 85 tumours) is better powered but then confounds sign with magnitude, which is the point of the test. That choice is the researcher's.
GPU estimate (section 10): about 13 GPU-hours on one A10 for all of (a), (b), (c) with VISTA-3D, D0 and BRIGHT-50, 16 with the Universal Model added, 19 to 22 with BRIGHT-25 as well (add 25% for margin); the wall time halves on two A10s.

This replaces the requested "download into its own directory on the persistent filesystem": the order was reversed because no instance existed when the work began.

## 4. Mask and label verification (before any registration result was trusted)
**5 registration patients** (`mask_check_5patients.json`): all 10 tumour files hold exactly {0, 1} (int16 or uint8; 770 to 336,018 voxels); the 10 organ masks are uint8 with labels up to 117 (the 117-class "total" task of TotalSegmentator; the paper says 104 VOIs); label 5 = 1.285 to 1.909 L, label 1 (spleen) 0.26 to 1.13 L, the liver lies to the patient's right of the spleen in 10 of 10, and 98.6% to 100% of every tumour lies inside label 5. **Cohort-wide** (339 tumour files, 461 phase-1/2 organ masks; `mask_check_cohort.json`):
- Tumour rule `> 0`: 330 of 339 files are exactly {0, 1}; **9 files in 8 patients (65, 72, 84, 92, 119, 121, 385, 439) use the value 2** (two of them mix 1 and 2). All nonzero voxels are counted as tumour; these patients are dropped in a sensitivity run (section 8).
- Liver: label 5 median 1.71 L; the liver is right of the spleen in 458 of 461 masks (two patients have no spleen label, one mask is empty); **439 phase 1 has no liver label at all** (24 labels present) and its patient falls into category E; several livers exceed 3 L (up to 5.36 L, patients 33 and 34).
- Tumour inside label 5: median 99.96%; **14 mask files below 0.9 (down to 0.26, patient 360)**: TotalSegmentator's liver misses part of some large tumours.
- **Label 64 = `portal_vein_and_splenic_vein`** (official TotalSegmentator class map, `map_to_binary.py`; 5 = liver, 63 = inferior vena cava); present in 458 of 461 masks, median 28 mL (max 75.5 mL), 0 voxels shared with label 5 by construction.
**Reference parenchyma** = label 5 minus all tumour voxels minus label 64 (`glassbox/audit/waw.py`, phantom-tested: two tumours, a bright portal vein in one shell and a hepatic vein; with the exclusion the contrasts are exactly +30.0 and -40.0 HU, with the vessel counted as liver the near tumour reads +26.1). **Parity with the HCC exp8 reference is approximate, not exact: label 64 is the main portal/splenic vein only; intrahepatic portal branches may remain in label 5, and hepatic veins stay in (as in exp8).** W1's vessel variants bound the effect on the sign: the CRML-CT "no vessel removal" reference flipped the contrast sign of 0 of 301 primary lesions (0 of 408 in all), and removing the hepatic veins flipped 2 of 301. The masked-phase contrast rerun with the explicit exclusion was **identical** to the first output (0 of 2,303 cells, no bright/dark flips), as expected for a single-valued label map; I had reported the first output's bright shares before this check and they stand.

## 6. Registration feasibility
**Method** (`scripts/w4_waw_register.py`): rigid (Euler3D) then affine, Mattes mutual information, 3 levels on a 3 mm grid cropped around the liver, metric restricted to the dilated target liver, initial translation by the liver centroids; a BSpline stage only if the affine liver Dice is below 0.92. **5-patient check** (3 masked on arterial, 2 on portal venous; seeded sample): liver Dice 0.78 / 0.91 / 0.97 / 0.93 / 0.86 unregistered, **0.951 / 0.965 / 0.969 / 0.946 / 0.960 after the affine**; tumour inside the target liver 0.978 to 1.000; volume ratio 0.98 to 1.00; tumour centroid shift 0.8 to 15.5 mm; visual overlays (`registration/overlay_*.png`) show the propagated mask on the lesion, including a very noisy arterial series (patient 514). Seconds per patient: 5 to 13.
**Pass threshold, fixed after the 5-patient check and before the cohort run** (`CONTRAST_RULES.md`): liver Dice at least 0.92; at least 90% of the propagated tumour inside the target liver; propagated / original tumour volume 0.85 to 1.15. The centroid shift is reported, not a criterion (it reflects breathing and positioning).
**Cohort run** (199 patients, `paired_host1/`): transforms: affine 191, affine plus BSpline 7, none 1 (the empty-liver patient); liver Dice after registration median 0.966, 5th percentile 0.935, minimum 0.529; tumour centroid shift median 3.1 mm, 90th percentile 11.5 mm, one outlier of 488 mm (a failed registration); **182 of 199 patients pass**.

## 9. Sign flips and the effective N (`sign_counts/`; rule and grid fixed before the cohort run; no detection outcome)
Sign concordance of the contrast on the 297 kept tumours (arterial / portal venous): **A+/P+ 162, A+/P- 80, A-/P- 50, A-/P+ 5**: the common flip is hyper-enhancement in the arterial phase and wash-out in the portal phase; the reverse almost never occurs. 85 tumours (72 patients) change sign at delta 0.
| delta (HU) \ rho | 0.33 | 0.5 | 0.67 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0: flips 85, comparable | 173; **N 44** | 120; **N 29** | 71; **N 19** |
| 5: flips 41, comparable | 141; **N 32** | **98; N 20 (primary)** | 57; **N 13** |
| 10: flips 14, comparable | 82; **N 14** | 65; **N 11** | 42; **N 8** |
(N = flips that are also comparable; drawn on arterial 13 and on portal venous 7 at the primary setting.) **Primary effective N = 20 tumours in 18 patients.** Sensitivity at the primary setting: liver eroded by 3 mm 30; median instead of mean of the shell 21; excluding the 8 patients with value-2 tumour files 19.
**Power (simulation, exact McNemar test, alpha 0.05, independent detection in the two phases; unadjusted for magnitude, so an upper bound for the planned regression):** N 20: 0.85 for detection 85% against 35% (a 50-point sign effect), 0.36 for 75% against 45% (30 points), 0.15 for 65% against 45% (20 points); N 41: 1.00 / 0.72 / 0.36; N 85: 1.00 / 0.98 / 0.72. The HCC-TACE-Seg gap was about 65 points, so a gap of that size would be detected; a modest effect would not.

## 10. Draft analysis plan (not a PREREG) and GPU estimate
(a) **Second-cohort replication**: the W3 / W5 gap definition (dark minus bright, detection coverage at least 0.10 and Dice) for VISTA-3D, the Universal Model (optional), D0, BRIGHT-50 and BRIGHT-25 on the drawn phase of the 181 patients with kept tumours; bright = contrast > 0 on that phase; patient-clustered bootstrap, margin 0.10 as in W3; the verdict rule written beforehand. (b) **Paired within-tumour test**: detection ~ sign + |contrast| + log diameter, patient-clustered (GEE or cluster bootstrap), on kept tumours with a contrast on both phases; the primary effective N (20) and the grid counts reported; the unadjusted paired test on all flips (85 or 41) as a descriptive companion; tumours inside the dead zone excluded and counted. (c) **D0 against BRIGHT-50 (and BRIGHT-25) on real arterial tumours**: detection and Dice on the 202 kept arterial-drawn tumours and on all kept tumours' arterial series, paired contrasts with patient-level bootstrap, the W5 rule style (CLOSES / DOES NOT CLOSE), plus the dose-response read-out.
GPU time (A10, per scan from earlier measured runs: VISTA-3D about 7 s, model A about 28 s, nnU-Net D0 and BRIGHT-50 about 56 s each; scans are the 398 arterial and portal-venous series of the 199 patients; add 25% for the different volume sizes): VISTA-3D + D0 + BRIGHT-50 on all 398 series about 13.2 h (16.5 h with margin); adding the Universal Model 16.3 h (20 h); adding BRIGHT-25 (56 s) to the three-model set 19.3 h (24 h), to the four-model set 22.4 h (28 h); D0 and BRIGHT-50 only on the 199 arterial series (analysis (c) alone) 6.2 h (7.7 h). Two A10s in parallel halve the wall time. Scoring, registration propagation of the masks to both phases and the bootstrap are CPU and fast. The detection reference on the other phase is the propagated mask of a QA-passed registration.

## 11. Provenance, limits and decisions needed
Scripts (all committed): `w4_waw_inventory.py`, `w4_waw_mask_check.py`, `w4_waw_contrast.py`, `w4_waw_register.py`, `w4_waw_paired_contrast.py`, `w4_waw_paired_instance.sh`, `w4_waw_crosscheck.py`, `w4_waw_sign_counts.py`, `glassbox/audit/waw.py`; hashes `code_hashes_paired.txt`; the instance run's seed, threads, workers, versions and hash file's hash in `paired_host1/run_meta.json` (registration sampling seed 1, ITK threads 2, 12 workers, Python 3.10.12, numpy 2.2.6, scipy 1.15.3, SimpleITK 2.5.6). Scouting fixes made after seeing some counts, all logged: the contrast reference (explicit label-64 exclusion, a no-op here) after the masked-phase shares had been seen; the field-of-view category and the restriction of QA criteria 2 and 3 to in-field tumours before the cohort run completed; the tolerant per-patient processing after an empty-liver patient was found.
Limits: one centre, one disease; phase labels are the dataset's (late arterial, portal venous), timing not verified; contrast of near-zero tumours is noisy (hence the dead zone); the propagated-mask contrast depends on a registration that fails for about 7% of patients; TotalSegmentator's liver is a model output and misses part of some large tumours; the sign-versus-magnitude test is observational (tumours are not edited); a tumour that is bright in one phase and dark in the other is a different kind of tumour from one that is not (wash-out hyper-vascular HCC), so within-tumour sign effects are not the same as the between-tumour gap of W3.
Decisions needed from the researcher: confirm GO for (a) and (c); decide whether (b) proceeds as a secondary at N 20 (power as above) or the primary rule is revisited as a new, pre-registered design; the model list and the GPU budget; whether the Universal Model is included; whether the 30 patients drawn on delayed or native phases are extracted for a descriptive extra.
