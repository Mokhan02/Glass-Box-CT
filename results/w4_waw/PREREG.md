## Question and predictions (stated before any outcome)
1. **Does the bright-versus-dark failure replicate in an independent cohort?** (a) **Prediction: yes** for VISTA-3D, the Universal Model (if included) and D0: the detection gap and the Dice gap (dark minus bright) are positive and at least the 0.10 margin.
2. **Does the augmentation's fix hold on real arterial tumours?** (c) **Prediction: BRIGHT-50 narrows the gap against D0 on the arterial series** (CLOSES on detection and Dice); BRIGHT-25 reported as the dose point. If it does not, the W5 result is specific to HCC-TACE-Seg or to its synthetic-edit family.
3. **Is it the sign of the contrast, with magnitude held, that matters?** (b) **Prediction: for the same tumour, detection is higher on the phase where it is brighter than the liver than on the phase where it is darker** (a positive sign effect) for D0 and VISTA-3D, smaller for BRIGHT-50. This test is observational (tumours are not edited) and its power is limited (below).

## Data, cohort, exclusions (all fixed in scouting)
WAW-TACE (Zenodo 12741586, CC BY 4.0, md5-verified), 199 patients with an arterial and a portal-venous series and a tumour mask on one of them; tumour = connected component of at least 10 mm longest axial diameter on the drawn phase; **329 tumours. Each is in exactly one category: K kept 297 (181 patients), Q registration QA failed 24, F propagated tumour outside the scanned field of view 4, E no usable registration 4.** Only K enters any analysis; the E/F/Q/K counts are reported with every result. Registration QA pass criteria (liver Dice >= 0.92; >= 90% of the propagated tumour inside the target liver; volume ratio 0.85 to 1.15) are the ones fixed before the cohort run; they are not tuned. **Bright = contrast > 0 HU** (tumour mean minus the 5 to 20 mm shell of the TotalSegmentator liver minus all tumour voxels minus the portal/splenic vein label 64), the project's shared function, **on the phase being scored**. **The value 2 in the tumour files of 8 patients (65, 72, 84, 92, 119, 121, 385, 439)** is the 3D Slicer label value of a segment, not another structure: the NRRD headers carry Slicer segmentation metadata (`Segment<k>_ID`, `_Name`, `_LabelValue`), and every file with the value 2 holds a segment named Segment_2 (label value 2) or, in two files (65_2_0 and 121_1_0), two segments (values 1 and 2) of one scene; a file exported from a scene's second segment alone keeps the value 2, and patient 65's second segment is exported both inside 65_2_0 and alone as 65_2_1 (the same 287 voxels), which the union (`> 0`) handles without double counting. All 8 patients' tumour voxels lie 96% to 100% inside the liver and their contrasts are ordinary; this reading is **inferred from the headers and not documented by the dataset authors**, so these patients stay in the primary and are dropped in a sensitivity analysis. The 30 patients with masks only on the delayed or native phase are outside the design. The detection reference on the phase a mask was not drawn on is the propagated mask of a QA-passed registration.

## Models, inference, scoring (the W3 / W5 pipelines, unchanged)
VISTA-3D class 26 through the shipped pipeline (reference row of W3); the CLIP-Driven Universal Model (**model A, included**) through its shipped pipeline, class 27; nnU-Net D0 (`nnUNetTrainerW3Seed`, `nnUNetv2_predict` defaults), BRIGHT-50 (seed 20261020) and BRIGHT-25 (**included, secondary in (c)**), with the W5 inference commands. Models are never pooled. **Detection = exactly the W3 Stage 1 definition: lesion coverage (the share of the lesion's voxels predicted positive, computed on the annotated slices by `glassbox.seg.w2_lib.lesion_metrics`, called as `glassbox.seg.w3_lib.lesion_rows` calls it) >= 0.10; Dice as `dice_full` of the same function; gap = dark minus bright.** The only extension is the lesion set: W3 scored the two largest lesions of a case, here every kept tumour of at least 10 mm is scored (the two-largest-per-series set is reported as a sensitivity). **, case-level (here patient-level) bootstrap, 1000 resamples, percentile 95% CIs, seeds from the SHA-256 of the contrast name. **New code, committed with unit tests and hashed before any GPU run:** a converter writing, per phase, the drawn mask and the propagated mask as NIfTI on the scan's grid; a scorer adapting `scripts/w3_modelD_eval.py` to these masks (same lesion definitions); the analysis script (below). **Alignment gate** (as W3, adapted): per series, the model's liver prediction against TotalSegmentator label 5 must agree in shape and need no flip, and the Dice-maximising translation must be <= 5 mm per axis (`glassbox.seg.w3_lib.shift_test`); a series that fails is flagged, kept in the primary, and dropped in a sensitivity analysis, as in W3. Sanity: each model's unhooked output must reproduce on a repeat of 3 series (coverage within 1e-3).

## Analyses and verdict rules
**(a) Replication (per model).** On the K tumours scored on their drawn phase: detection gap and Dice gap with patient-clustered CIs. **Label per metric (W3 rule, margin 0.10):** GAP if the CI lower bound > 0 and the point estimate >= 0.10; NO GAP if the CI upper bound < 0.10; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise; model label "GAP (detection and Dice)", "(detection only)", "(Dice only)", "NO GAP" or "INCONCLUSIVE". **Strata reported, not pooled:** tumours drawn on the arterial phase (242 bright / 55 dark by arterial contrast, among all K) and on the portal-venous phase, because the bright share differs sharply (85.7% against 50.5% on the drawn phase). Size-adjusted gap as in W3.
**(c) D0 against BRIGHT-50 and BRIGHT-25 on real arterial tumours.** Every K tumour scored on the ARTERIAL series (drawn mask where drawn there, propagated mask otherwise), bright or dark by its arterial contrast; Delta = gap(BRIGHT-50) - gap(D0), paired patient-level bootstrap (the same resampled patients for all arms); **CLOSES if the CI upper bound < 0 and the point estimate <= -0.10; DOES NOT CLOSE if the CI lower bound > -0.10; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise**, per metric; overall label "CONFIRMED (detection and Dice / detection only / Dice only)", "NOT CONFIRMED" or "INCONCLUSIVE" exactly as W5. BRIGHT-25 against D0 and against BRIGHT-50 reported with the same machinery, **descriptive, no verdict**. Secondary: detection rate among the bright arterial tumours, BRIGHT-50 minus D0 (paired).
**(b) Real-phase brightness effect: conditional logistic regression with the tumour as stratum (redesigned before any outcome).**
*Why the redesign:* sign and phase are confounded in the flipping tumours (80 of 85 are bright on the arterial and dark on the portal-venous phase), so a contrast of "bright phase against dark phase" mostly compares arterial with portal-venous. The new design separates them: the **non-flipping tumours (212 of 297, bright on both phases or dark on both) identify the phase effect**, and the **flipping tumours identify the sign effect beyond the phase effect**.
*Population:* ALL K tumours with a contrast on both phases (297 tumours in 181 patients); each contributes its two observations (arterial and portal-venous series), detection = coverage >= 0.10 of the drawn or propagated mask.
*Model:* detected ~ phase + sign + |contrast| + **is_propagated** (+ log diameter, because the diameter of the propagated mask differs slightly from the drawn one; reported with and without it), the tumour as the stratum, so every tumour-level factor (size, location, appearance, model-specific difficulty) cancels. Coding: phase = 1 on the arterial series; **sign = 1 if the tumour-minus-liver contrast on that series is > 0 HU** (the primary coding, no dead zone; a version that drops tumours with |contrast| < 5 HU on either phase is a sensitivity); |contrast| in units of 10 HU; **is_propagated = 1 on the series that carries the registered mask, 0 on the series where the mask was drawn** (for an arterial-drawn tumour the portal-venous series is propagated and the reverse). With two observations per tumour the conditional likelihood involves only the **discordant tumours** (detected on exactly one phase) and reduces to an ordinary logistic regression of "detected on the arterial and missed on the portal-venous series" on the within-tumour differences, whose intercept is the phase effect; the sign coefficient is the effect of the contrast being positive on the series where the tumour is detected, with phase, magnitude and mask provenance held. **is_propagated is identified by the arterial-drawn tumours against the portal-venous-drawn ones** (202 against 95 kept tumours, 115 against 66 patients); it is not collinear with sign among the flippers either (A-bright/P-dark flippers: 57 arterial-drawn and 23 portal-venous-drawn; A-dark/P-bright: 2 and 3). **Expected direction of the bias it removes:** a propagated mask is an estimate of the tumour on the other phase (boundary and registration error of a few millimetres), so detection (coverage at least 0.10 of a slightly misplaced reference) is expected to be UNDER-estimated on the propagated series, an OR below 1 for is_propagated. Because most tumours were drawn on the arterial phase (202 of 297) and most flippers are arterial-drawn, the portal-venous series is mostly the propagated one: without the covariate the phase OR is biased upward (arterial detected more) and the sign OR is biased upward as well (the dark, portal-venous series of A-bright/P-dark flippers carries the less accurate reference), i.e. anti-conservatively. With the covariate that bias is removed to the extent the penalty is homogeneous (it is not tested to be).
*Primary statistic:* **the sign coefficient as an odds ratio (OR > 1: more likely to be detected on the series where it is brighter than the liver)**, with a patient-clustered bootstrap percentile 95% CI (2000 resamples of patients, seed from the SHA-256 of `w4_b_<model>`); the sandwich Wald CI is reported beside it; a fit that does not converge (separation) is replaced for that replicate by a Jeffreys-penalised fit and the number of such replicates is reported. **Primary model VISTA-3D**; the same analysis for D0, the Universal Model (A) and BRIGHT-50, each with its own label, never pooled; BRIGHT-25 descriptive.
*Verdict per model:* **REAL-PHASE BRIGHTNESS EFFECT if the CI of the sign OR lies entirely above 1; OPPOSITE EFFECT if it lies entirely below 1; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise.** Prediction (stated now): REAL-PHASE BRIGHTNESS EFFECT for VISTA-3D, D0 and A; a smaller OR for BRIGHT-50.
*Reported with every result:* the number of tumours that inform each coefficient (**discordant tumours in total; among them non-flipping tumours (phase effect), flipping A-bright/P-dark tumours and flipping A-dark/P-bright tumours (sign effect)**), the phase OR and the magnitude coefficient with CIs.
*Power (simulation of this design, including is_propagated, on the real covariates of the 297 kept tumours; 600 simulations per scenario; tumour random intercept SD 1.5, mean detection 70%; phase OR 1 or 1.5, propagated-mask OR 1 or 0.7, magnitude effect 0.3 per 10 HU; patient-clustered Wald CI; `results/w4_waw/paired_logit_power.json`):* about 98 to 104 tumours are discordant, 28 to 52 of them sign-flippers. **Probability that the CI of the sign OR excludes 1 in the predicted direction: true OR 1 (null): 0.02 to 0.035; OR 3: 0.59 to 0.66; OR 5: 0.88 to 0.93; OR 10: 0.99 to 1.00; OR 25: 1.00.** For scale, the HCC-TACE-Seg bright and dark detection rates of VISTA-3D (50% against 96.5%) correspond to an OR of about 27, and of D0 (21% against 86%) about 23. So the design is adequate for effects of the size seen before and has about 60% power for an OR of 3; an INCONCLUSIVE result at a modest OR is not evidence of absence.
*Sensitivity (the committed design and others, reported, none chosen from):* **the effective-N design of v1: the 20 flip-and-comparable tumours (delta 5 HU, rho 0.5) with the within-tumour difference in detection, bright phase minus dark phase, patient-clustered CI, margin 0.20 (SIGN EFFECT if the CI lower bound > 0 and the point >= 0.20; NO SIGN EFFECT if the CI upper bound < 0.20; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise; power 0.85 / 0.36 / 0.15 for 50 / 30 / 20-point effects)**; the full delta x rho grid (N 8 to 44); all 85 sign flips; the dead-zone sign coding; signed contrast as a continuous covariate; without log diameter; without |contrast|.
**Sensitivity analyses (all reported, none chosen from):** liver eroded by 3 mm; shell median instead of mean; **the propagated mask dilated by 2 mm (physical units, ball structuring element), applied to the propagated series only, with the detection and every verdict of (a), (b) and (c) recomputed and a list of any verdict that changes**; the two-largest-lesions-per-series set of W3; excluding the 8 patients whose tumour files hold the value 2 (what the value means is explained above); including Q-category tumours; excluding series that fail the alignment gate; tumours at least 15 mm.

## Limits, named in advance
One centre and one disease (TACE-treated HCC), the same disease as HCC-TACE-Seg, so this is a replication in a second cohort, not generalisation across diseases. Masks were drawn on the phase where the tumour was most conspicuous (phase-selection confound; hence the strata and (b)). The contrast of a tumour near 0 HU is noisy (the dead zone). A propagated mask is an estimate (liver Dice after registration median 0.966, 7% of patients fail QA); TotalSegmentator's liver is a model output that misses part of some large tumours, and label 64 covers only the main portal/splenic vein, so the exp8 parity is approximate (W1 vessel variants: 0 of 301 sign flips). Tumours that wash out (bright arterial, dark portal) are a particular kind of HCC: a within-tumour sign effect is not the same quantity as the between-tumour gap of W3. No multiplicity correction: (a), (c) and (b) are three separately labelled families (b has its own pre-stated power), and the model-level labels are never pooled. **The phase effect in (b) is assumed homogeneous across the flipping and the non-flipping tumours (the non-flippers identify it, the flippers are then assumed to share it); this cannot be tested here, and the design would be biased if the arterial-versus-portal-venous detection difference differed between wash-out tumours and the others.** The 5 tumours that are dark on the arterial and bright on the portal-venous phase, the only ones that break the collinearity of phase and sign, are reported descriptively per model (their detection on each series, contrast on each series, diameter, drawn phase). VISTA-3D private-data caveat: unlisted in-house training data cannot be excluded from the public record. D0, BRIGHT-50 and BRIGHT-25 are single training runs with no baseline seed-variance estimate (D0-seed2 was not run).

## Erratum 1 (2026-11-04, after the primary analysis was run; the text above is NOT edited)
**The predicted direction of analysis (b) was mis-specified in PREREG v2.** Prediction 3, the verdict labels of (b) (REAL-PHASE BRIGHTNESS EFFECT if the CI of the sign OR lies entirely above 1; OPPOSITE EFFECT if entirely below) and the labels of the v1 effective-N sensitivity (SIGN EFFECT for a positive bright-minus-dark detection difference) state the sign effect as "detection HIGHER on the phase where the tumour is brighter than the liver (OR > 1)". That contradicts prediction 1 (and W3, W5), where bright tumours are detected LESS (gap = dark minus bright > 0). The hypothesis the test was meant to examine corresponds to OR < 1. I drafted the text and it was approved before any outcome; the error was found when the results were read (DEVIATIONS 24).
**Handling.** (1) **The pre-registered labels are reported exactly as written** (VISTA-3D INCONCLUSIVE, D0 INCONCLUSIVE, Universal Model OPPOSITE EFFECT, BRIGHT-50 REAL-PHASE BRIGHTNESS EFFECT; v1 design NO SIGN EFFECT / INCONCLUSIVE) and none is relabelled. (2) **The corrected-direction reading (OR < 1: bright detected less within the same tumour) and the descriptive v1 contrast (bright minus dark detection in the 21 flip-and-comparable tumours) are post hoc and exploratory.** (3) The conditional-logit fits of (b) are fragile: with about 70 discordant tumours and five covariates, 1500 to 2000 of the 2000 bootstrap replicates per model needed the Jeffreys-penalised fit, the log-diameter coefficient is not identifiable in practice, and verdicts change with the specification and the sensitivities (W4_RESULTS.md). **(b) is therefore not used for any confirmatory claim**; the confirmatory results of W4 are (a) and (c).
**BRIGHT-25 was not run in W4** (Amendment 1, compute); its W4 rows in (c) and (b) do not exist.
