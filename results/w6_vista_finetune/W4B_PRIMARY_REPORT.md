## 3. What this does and does not say
The bright-augmented fine-tune removes the within-tumour bright penalty and closes the dark-minus-bright gap on real arterial tumours of an independent centre, while a fine-tune with the same procedure but darkened tumours keeps the failure: the effect is the bright edit, not fine-tuning or the intensity edit as such. Limits: **single runs** (no seed-variance estimate until BRIGHT-FT-s2 is scored), **PLAIN-FT is not yet available** (so the separation of "any fine-tune" from "edit procedure" rests on SHAM-FT alone), one centre and one disease, **forgetting not yet judged** (the guard is the next section of the report; the first BRIGHT kidney numbers are lower than the baseline's), the equivalence label S3 is borderline-robust (one sensitivity changes it), and the within-tumour estimator assumes the phase effect is the same in flipping and non-flipping tumours.
