# Deviations for the trust-layer analyses

## 1. Which gated-recalibration result is quoted (decided after both results were seen)
The sensitivity analysis (nested CV; not pre-registered) was requested with the instruction to quote "the more conservative of the two results". Before running it I wrote, in `scripts/gated_recal_nested.py`, that the more conservative would be the one with the larger gated mean absolute error. When both results existed that rule selected the pre-registered numbers (mean absolute error 20.6% vs 19.8% nested), but those are the more favourable numbers on the criteria that actually decide shipping: over-estimation of at least 20% is 3.4% pre-registered vs 7.9% [3.4, 14.6] nested, and the nested analysis does not meet the pre-registered shipping rule (gated minus always-recalibrated over-estimation -0.045, 95% CI -0.124 to +0.034, includes zero). My rule was a poor definition of "conservative", so it was replaced, with this note, by: quote the nested analysis, which is also the stricter test because nothing about the held-out fold touches any choice. Both results stay in `results/SUMMARY.md` and the quote file records both. The change was made on 2026-10-12, after both results had been read.

## 2. The phase heuristic is removed from the product's verdicts and main UI (decided after seeing its evaluation)
PREREG section A defined a blind-spot warning that fired on `arterial-like` OR measured contrast > 0, and the first product build used the phase estimate in verdict reasons and showed a phase card. The pre-registered evaluation then found agreement with the reference-contrast sign at chance level (kappa 0.04, 95% CI -0.16 to 0.24) and a miss-rate difference of +11 points with a CI of -9 to +31. On 2026-10-12 the estimate was removed from verdicts, reasons, the API summary and the scan panel; the bright-tumour warning now uses the measured tumour-minus-liver contrast only. The estimate remains in the audit record only, labelled "unvalidated heuristic (agreement with reference contrast sign at chance level, kappa 0.04)". The pre-registered analysis itself is unchanged and its results stand in `results/trust/PHASE_RESULTS.md`.
