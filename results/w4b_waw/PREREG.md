## 1. Why this exists
W4 analysis (b) was pre-registered with the wrong direction (OR > 1 = better detection when bright; PREREG Erratum 1, DEVIATIONS 24). The hypothesis that W3, W4 (a) and W5 are about is: **for the same tumour, detection is LOWER on the series where it is brighter than the liver.** W4b states that direction, a more stable estimator and its decision rules BEFORE any W4b model has produced a result. The W4 data supplied only the covariates (patients, drawn phase, contrast signs) and, for the power simulations, plausible effect sizes (the W4 v1 contrast of -0.3 to -0.6 in the non-augmented models). Disclosed, not hidden.

## 4. Detection, cohort, scoring (unchanged from W4)
The 297 kept tumours of `results/w4_waw/paired_instances` (181 patients), both series scored; detection = lesion coverage >= 0.10 (W3 Stage 1); sign = contrast > 0 HU on that series (no dead zone; the 5 HU dead zone is a sensitivity); alignment gate and sensitivities as in W4. WAW-TACE reference liver masks: TotalSegmentator `total` (see section 3); used only for the contrast.

## 5. The estimand and the estimator
**Tumour classes** by contrast sign: F1 = bright on the arterial and not bright on the portal-venous series (81 tumours); NF = same sign on both (211); F2 = the reverse (5; excluded from the estimator, listed per model).
**Estimand in plain detection-probability units.** theta = [P(detected on the portal-venous series) - P(detected on the arterial series), among F1 tumours] - [the same difference among NF tumours], standardised over the drawn-phase strata (arterial-drawn / portal-venous-drawn) with the F1 stratum shares. **theta = +0.30 means: after removing what the phase alone does (the portal-venous minus arterial change in detection probability seen in tumours whose brightness does not change), the dark series of a wash-out tumour is detected 30 percentage points more often than its bright series.** theta > 0 is the predicted direction ("bright penalty"); theta = 0 no within-tumour effect. In the standardised estimator the stratum weights are fixed by the F1 shares (so the propagated-mask asymmetry is held fixed). Identifying assumption, stated as a limit: **the phase effect is the same in flipping and in non-flipping tumours (untestable here).**
**Inference.** Patient-clustered percentile bootstrap, 2000 resamples (seed = first 8 hex digits of the SHA-256 of `w4b_<test name>`; the same resampled patients for all models in a paired test), 95% CI. (A risk-difference estimator replaces W4's conditional logit, whose fits needed the penalised version in 1500 to 2000 of 2000 bootstrap replicates and whose log-diameter term was not identifiable.)

## 6. Tests, labels, predictions (direction fixed now)
**PRIMARY (one test).** Same tumours, paired patients: **d = theta(BRIGHT-FT) - theta(SHAM-FT)**; one-sided at alpha 0.025 (the 95% bootstrap CI upper bound below 0). **Label BRIGHT-FT REDUCES THE PENALTY if the CI upper bound < 0 and d <= -0.10; DOES NOT REDUCE if the CI lower bound > -0.10; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise.** Prediction: REDUCES. (Requires BRIGHT-FT and SHAM-FT; with only one of them there is no primary test.)
**SECONDARY, each its own label (paired, same rule, no multiplicity correction, none a verdict of W4b's primary):** (S1) theta(BRIGHT-FT) - theta(PLAIN-FT); (S2) theta(BRIGHT-DEC) - theta(SHAM-FT) (the decoder-only arm; prediction by the W6 hypothesis: REDUCES); (S3) **NO PENALTY for BRIGHT-FT (equivalence): the whole 95% CI of theta(BRIGHT-FT) lies within (-0.25, +0.25)**; if not met, INCONCLUSIVE. Prediction: NO PENALTY (but see power).
**Per-model labels (all models, own verdict):** BRIGHT PENALTY if the CI lower bound > 0 and theta >= 0.20; REVERSED if the CI upper bound < 0 and theta <= -0.20; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. **For the public models only these three labels exist: a null public result is INCONCLUSIVE and is never called "no penalty"** (the power at a true sign OR of 0.37 is 31%, below). Predictions: SHAM-FT, PLAIN-FT, TotalSegmentator (if run): BRIGHT PENALTY.
**Secondary descriptive (never a verdict):** (i) W4 (a) gaps per model; (ii) the conditional-logit sign OR (phase, sign, |contrast|, is_propagated; no log diameter; penalised in every replicate so one estimator is used throughout; predicted OR < 1 in penalty models; the penalised-replicate share reported); (iii) the v1 contrast (flip-and-comparable tumours, bright minus dark detection); (iv) the 5 F2 tumours per model; (v) **the W4 models (baseline VISTA, Universal Model, D0, BRIGHT-50) under the new estimator, labelled ALREADY SEEN.** Anything that reads the other way is reported as REVERSED / INCONCLUSIVE with no relabelling.

## 7. Power (CPU simulation of these estimators on the real covariates; synthetic outcomes; 200 to 300 simulations x 200 to 300 bootstrap draws)
Generating model: tumour and patient random intercepts (total SD about 1.5), phase OR 0.15, propagated-mask OR 0.7, dark portal-venous non-propagated detection about 0.75; the two models of a pair share the tumour and patient effects and add an independent model term (SD 0.7).
**Single-model labels (`w4b_did_power.json`):**
| true sign OR (bright vs dark) | mean theta | P(BRIGHT PENALTY label) | P(NO PENALTY, margin 0.20) | P(NO PENALTY, margin 0.25) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | 0.00 | 0.003 | 0.48 | **0.78** |
| 0.61 | 0.08 | 0.05 | 0.37 | 0.62 |
| **0.37** | 0.17 | **0.31** | 0.07 | 0.19 |
| 0.10 (what the W4 non-augmented models showed) | 0.36 | 0.99 | 0.00 | 0.00 |
**Primary test, BRIGHT-FT minus SHAM-FT, SHAM-FT at OR 0.10 (`w4b_primary_power.json`):** BRIGHT-FT fully without penalty (OR 1.0): P(REDUCES) **0.94**, mean d -0.35; half-way (OR 0.61): 0.82; weak reduction (OR 0.37): **0.53**; no difference between the arms (OR 0.10): 0.025 (the one-sided alpha).
**Consequences, stated now:** a penalty of the W4 size is found 99% of the time in a model that has it; a smaller one (OR 0.37) 31% of the time, so **a public model that shows no penalty is INCONCLUSIVE, never evidence of none**; the equivalence claim S3 can be reached with only 78% probability even if BRIGHT-FT's true effect is exactly zero; the primary test detects a complete removal with 94% and a partial one (OR 0.37) only about half of the time. INCONCLUSIVE is not evidence of absence.
