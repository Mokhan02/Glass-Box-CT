# Deviations / notes for the mechanistic follow-up

## 1. Sign convention of the "same channels for bright vs dark" rule (found after seeing A's result)
PREREG A defined D_j = detected minus missed and B_j = bright minus dark, then required a POSITIVE Spearman correlation between them for "same channels". Bright tumours have LOWER logits than dark ones (B_j sums negative) while detected voxels have HIGHER logits than missed ones (D_j sums positive), so a real overlap shows up as a strongly NEGATIVE correlation, which the rule as written cannot count. The literal pre-registered verdict is reported unchanged ("same channels" = false). The sign-aware reading (|Spearman| with the opposite sign, i.e. bright tumours look like missed voxels in the same channels) is POST HOC and is labelled exploratory wherever it appears. No other rule was changed. Written after A's result had been read (A ran 2026-10-11 before this note).

## 2. POST HOC exploratory stratified check for A (requested after A's result was read; written before this check was run)
Not pre-registered. Question: is the bright-vs-dark overlap in A just the miss rate (bright tumours have more missed voxels, and missed voxels are low in those channels), or does brightness lower the same channels within voxels of the same outcome?
- Within DETECTED tumour voxels only, and within MISSED tumour voxels only: per-case mean contribution per channel (a case enters a stratum with >= 20 such voxels), bright-case mean minus dark-case mean = S_j (per stratum). Patient-bootstrap 95% CIs (2000 resamples, seed 1) per channel and for the stratum total (the sum over channels = the stratum's mean logit gap).
- Share of each stratum's gap carried by the fixed channels 36, 20, 10, 22, 18, 30, 17, 7 (the all-data top 8 from A; chosen on all data, so the share is optimistic).
- Interpretation rule, fixed now: brightness has its OWN effect on those channels if, in BOTH strata, the stratum total gap has a CI excluding zero on the negative side and the fixed-8 share is >= 0.50. If the stratum totals' CIs include zero, the overlap in A "mostly reflects miss rate". Anything else is reported as mixed.
- Exploratory; not entered into SUMMARY.md as a pre-registered result.

## 3. D: lesions with no cell in a region were not excluded (found when reading D's results)
PREREG D said a lesion with no cell in a region at a layer is excluded from that layer-region and counted. The implementation did not do this: if a region had no cell (>= 50% fraction) in a window, no patch was applied for it, and the lesion still entered the pooled ratio as "nothing restored". This matters only for coarse grids: at the bottleneck (16-voxel = 24 mm cells) a 10 mm shell rarely fills half a cell, so the R2 (shell only) and, to a lesser degree, R3 results at the bottleneck are biased toward zero and are largely uninformative. R1 (tumour plus 10 mm) always contains cells for eligible lesions of this size; mid (6 mm cells) and decoder_F (1.5 mm) are not affected materially. The number of excluded lesions per layer-region was not recorded, so this cannot be recomputed from the saved CSVs. Reported as is; nothing re-run.

## 4. B: first run invalid (class rows swapped), caught by the pre-registered sanity check; re-run
The first B run (2026-10-11, ~11 min) indexed the two output rows the wrong way round (row 0 is the class-1 liver logit, row 1 the class-26 tumour logit; the script used row 0 as tumour). The pre-registered check "no-hook run reproduces the stored class-26 logits" reported a max difference of 24.6 logit and failed, which stopped the analysis before any B result was looked at. A diagnostic (unhooked run vs stored logits) reproduces exactly (0 inside the ROI, 0.016 outside from fp16 storage). Fixed (`scripts/mech_b_run.py`), the invalid outputs deleted, B re-run in full. Eraser parameters and the probe check (CPU, `mech_b_fit.py`) are unaffected.

## 5. POST HOC exploratory bridge between B and D (requested after B and D were read; written before it was run)
Not pre-registered. Bright-version activations were not saved by D (it recorded only the DARK activations), so the forward passes for the 62 Arm A pairs were re-run, with a hook that only reads the mid-encoder (`layers[2]["blocks"]`) output; nothing else changed (same preprocessing, edit and window positions as D; the dark and bright coverages are taken from D's own CSVs).
- Concept score: for a tumour cell, t = a . (x - mu) / (a . d) using the fold's LEACE parameters (the held-out fold of that case; `cache/mech_b/erasers.npz`), i.e. the amount of the erased tumour-vs-liver direction present (units: multiples of the erased direction d). Cells = 32^3-grid mid-encoder cells of every window whose lesion fraction is >= 0.5 (lesion = GT lesion mask carried through the preprocessing); lesions with no such cell are excluded and counted. Per tumour: the mean of t over those cells, in the dark and in the bright version.
- Reported (exploratory): mean paired difference (bright minus dark) per tumour with a patient-bootstrap 95% CI (2000 resamples of cases, seed 1) and an exact two-sided sign test; the Pearson and Spearman correlation across tumours between the projection drop (dark minus bright) and the coverage drop (dark minus bright coverage, D's values), with bootstrap CIs; both on all complete tumours and on the 32 eligible ones from D.
- Interpretation rule fixed now: the projection "tracks the brightness effect" if the paired difference has a CI below zero AND the Spearman correlation CI is above zero on the eligible set. No other claim will be made.
