# Pre-registration: mechanistic follow-up (A direct logit attribution, B erasure, C steering, D counterfactual patching)

Written and committed before ANY of these experiments was run. Research use only. Hard stop: 2026-10-15. Order: A (CPU) first, then D, B, C on the GPU; if time runs out, stop after B and report what is done. Anything that departs from this file goes in `results/mech/DEVIATIONS.md` with its timing.

## Common to all
- Cohort: the 89-case clean cohort (UID-route cases plus RESOLVED re-conversions; same list as the counterfactual and the re-fit). Folds: cases sorted by id, fold = index % 5 (identical to `results/readout_refit/`). Every fit (LEACE, probes, steering directions, alpha, thresholds) uses training folds only and is applied to held-out folds only. Patient-bootstrap: 2000 resamples of patients (cases), percentile 95% CI, seed 1.
- "Bright" = case-level tumour-minus-liver contrast > 0 HU, "dark" = <= 0 HU (`contrast_hu` stored in `cache/rr/*.npz`).
- Spaces: the preprocessed 1.5 mm RAS grid (same as the re-fit; labels from `cache/rr/*.npz` key `L`: 0 background, 1 liver, 2 tumour, 3 unknown slice), annotated slices only. Exception: D uses the original-space coverage of the counterfactual (`measure()` in `scripts/contrast_cf_run.py`).
- Lesions = GT RECIST targets (>= 10 mm longest axial diameter, 2 largest per case). Lesion coverage = fraction of the lesion's voxels predicted positive (logit > 0); detected = coverage >= 10%. Lesion Dice = Dice between the lesion and the prediction inside the lesion dilated by 10 mm (as in the counterfactual). Case Dice, sum-of-diameters (SLD) relative error and over-estimation (>= +20%) are defined as in the re-fit.
- Interventions act inside the network by forward hooks; the real sliding-window inference (128^3, overlap 0.3, prompts [1, 26]) runs end to end. Hook points: mid = output of `image_encoder.encoder.layers[2]["blocks"]` (192 ch, 32^3 per window, stride 4 voxels); bottleneck = output of `layers[4]["blocks"]` (768 ch, 8^3, stride 16); decoder_F = output of `class_head.image_post_mapping` (48 ch, 128^3). Hooks on the stage blocks replace the tensor that is both stored as the skip and passed to the next stage, so every downstream computation sees the intervention.
- Ground truth is never used at test time by any intervention (ROI = predicted liver, class-1 logit > 0, dilated 10 mm, exactly as in the re-fit; `roi_idx` in `cache/rr`).
- A run with no hook is repeated first and must reproduce the stored class-26 logits in `cache/rr` (max abs difference < 0.05 logit); otherwise stop and log.

## A. Direct logit attribution (CPU; saved voxel-level decoder_F from the re-fit)
Class-26 logit at a voxel = w . F, w = M(E_c[26]) (48-d, `cache/rr/w_orig.npy`), no bias. Per-channel contribution c_j = w_j F_j (they sum to the logit; verified on every case).
- Groups (GT tumour voxels in annotated slices inside the ROI): detected (original logit > 0) vs missed (<= 0), each x bright vs dark; liver reference = GT liver (label 1) non-tumour voxels. Per-case group means first (a case enters a group only with >= 20 voxels), then the mean over cases; patient-bootstrap CIs.
- Missed-vs-detected gap: D_j = mean c_j over detected minus over missed voxels (per case when a case has both, then mean over such cases); sum_j D_j = total logit gap.
- **Concentration (pre-registered):** channels are ranked by D_j (descending) on training folds; k = 8 (one sixth of 48). The held-out share = (sum over the 8 training-selected channels of D_j) / (sum over all channels of D_j), computed on held-out folds and pooled by case. "Concentrated" iff the held-out share >= 0.50 (point estimate); "clearly concentrated" iff also its CI lower bound >= 0.50. The minimum k reaching 50% on all data is reported as a descriptive number (optimistic).
- **Same channels for bright vs dark:** B_j = mean c_j over all GT tumour voxels of bright cases minus of dark cases. Reported: (i) the share of the bright-minus-dark logit gap carried by the same 8 training-selected channels, (ii) the Spearman correlation across the 48 channels between D_j (missed vs detected) and B_j with patient-bootstrap CI. "Same channels" iff the Spearman CI excludes zero on the positive side AND the share in (i) >= 0.50.
- Also: cosine between w and the decoder_F probe direction (logistic probe, tumour vs shell+liver sampled voxels as in the re-fit; per fold on training folds, and on all cases).

## B. Erasure (necessity) at the mid-encoder
- Eraser: LEACE (least-squares concept erasure, closed form, covariance ridge 1e-4 x mean eigenvalue) fitted on training-fold mid tokens from `cache/vp/trained` (192-d pooled tokens, concept = token is tumour vs liver; the token tables restricted to the 89 clean cases). Applied as an affine map on the 192-ch stage-2 feature map in every window (it commutes with the average pooling used for tokens).
- Controls (same rank 1): (a) a random unit direction erased by orthogonal projection through the training-token mean (seed = fold); (b) LEACE for a concept that is only local HU statistics (cell mean HU, `mid_hu14[:, 0]`, continuous).
- Population: lesions the ORIGINAL model detects (coverage >= 10% in the unhooked run). Outcome per lesion: change in lesion Dice and in coverage; and the fraction still detected. Liver check: per-case Dice of class 1 (prediction logit > 0 vs GT liver = labels 1 and 2) before and after.
- Probe check: logistic probe (L2, standardised) trained on erased training-fold tokens and tested on erased held-out tokens; "near chance" iff held-out AUROC upper CI <= 0.60 (the same probe before erasure is reported).
- **Decision:** "USED" iff the mean change in lesion Dice under LEACE is below zero with a CI excluding zero AND the paired difference (LEACE minus random) and (LEACE minus HU-only) each has a CI entirely below zero. Otherwise "NOT NECESSARY". If the mean liver Dice drops by more than 0.05 under LEACE, the verdict is reported as "erasure too blunt" in addition. If the post-erasure probe is not near chance, the run is reported as not testing necessity.

## C. Steering (sufficiency) at the mid-encoder and at the bottleneck (not decoder_F)
- Direction: logistic probe (tumour vs liver tokens, standardised features, direction = w / sigma, unit-normalised to u) fitted on training-fold tokens of that layer. Scale unit dmu = mean projection of tumour tokens minus of liver tokens on u (training folds). Step added to every feature vector in the ROI cells (ROI fraction >= 0.5 at the layer's stride): alpha x dmu x u.
- Alpha grid {0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5}. Per fold, alpha is chosen on a fixed random subset of 20 training cases (seed = fold) as the value maximising mean case Dice there.
- Comparator: cross-validated threshold shift inside the ROI, t in {0, -0.25, ..., -5.0}, chosen on the same 20 training cases so that the mean healthy-liver false-positive volume (predicted tumour inside GT liver, non-tumour, > 10 mm from GT tumour; cm^3) equals the steering run's on those cases (closest grid value; ties: less negative). Primary matching is by false-positive volume because over-estimation counts (>= 20%) on 20 cases are too few to match; the held-out over-estimation rates of both arms are reported, and "beats" additionally requires steering's held-out over-estimation rate not to exceed the threshold arm's by more than 5 percentage points.
- Controls at the chosen alpha and equal step norm: (a) random unit directions (one per case, seeded); (b) the mean-difference direction between missed and detected GT-tumour tokens on training folds (from `cache/vp/trained`, `pred_cov < 0.5` vs `>= 0.5`).
- Outcomes (held-out folds, 89 cases): case Dice, lesion detection, SLD median relative error, % over-estimated >= 20%; overall and bright vs dark.
- **Decision (per layer):** "BEATS THRESHOLD" iff the per-case Dice gain of steering over the matched threshold arm has a CI excluding zero on the positive side AND the gain over the random-direction control has a CI excluding zero on the positive side AND the over-estimation condition holds. A CI entirely below zero is reported as "WORSE THAN THRESHOLD". Otherwise "EQUIVALENT TO THRESHOLD".

## D. Counterfactual patching (brightness path)
- Pairs: the 62 Arm A lesions of `results/contrast_cf` (dark tumour found by the model; the pre-registered edit makes the lesion bright, same `edit_lesion`/target as `edits.csv`, cond = edit). DARK = the unedited scan, BRIGHT = the edited scan. Both go through the identical preprocessing; same shape and window positions are asserted.
- Patch: run BRIGHT; at layer L in {mid, bottleneck, decoder_F}, inside a region, replace the activation of every window by the DARK run's activation of the same window. Regions (lesion mask carried through the same transforms, nearest): (R1) dilated tumour = lesion plus a 10 mm shell; (R2) shell only = 0 < distance <= 10 mm; (R3) far context = distance > 10 mm (the rest of the volume). Cell masks at coarse strides use the region fraction >= 0.5; a lesion with no cell in a region at a layer is excluded from that layer-region and counted.
- Control: R1 filled with the DARK activations of an unrelated Arm A case (from a fixed pool of 5 cases, never the same case), taken at the same voxel indices of the preprocessed grid (activation volumes assembled by window averaging; cells outside the donor volume keep the BRIGHT values).
- Eligibility: lesions whose own re-run satisfies coverage(DARK) - coverage(BRIGHT) >= 0.20 (a re-run of BRIGHT must agree with `edits.csv` within 0.05, else the lesion is flagged and reported).
- Outcome: pooled restored fraction = sum(cov_patched - cov_bright) / sum(cov_dark - cov_bright) over eligible lesions (original-space `measure()` coverage), and the same with the mean class-26 logit over the lesion core instead of coverage; patient-bootstrap CI (lesions clustered by case).
- **Readout:** per region, the earliest layer (mid < bottleneck < decoder_F) whose restored fraction is >= 0.50 (point estimate; CI reported) is "where the brightness effect arises" for that region; a layer-region whose CI upper bound is below 0.50 is reported as not restoring. The unrelated-case control must restore less than R1 at the same layer for any R1 result to be read as specific.
