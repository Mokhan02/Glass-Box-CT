## W4: WAW-TACE (Zenodo 12741586, CC BY 4.0), summary
**Design (PREREG v2 + Amendment 1 + Erratum 1; DEVIATIONS 18 to 24).** 297 kept tumours (at least 10 mm) in 181 patients with an arterial and a portal-venous CT series each; the tumour mask drawn on one phase was propagated to the other by registration (liver Dice median 0.966; masks and contrasts from the same registration), so every tumour is scored on both phases. Models: VISTA-3D, the Universal Model (research use), D0 (nnU-Net without contrast augmentation) and BRIGHT-50 (with it); BRIGHT-25 was not run (compute). Detection = lesion coverage >= 0.10 (the W3 definition); gap = dark minus bright (positive: bright tumours detected less); bright = tumour brighter than the liver on the scored series. Reference figures and sensitivities: `results/w4_waw/W4_RESULTS.md`.

**Confirmatory results**
- **(a) The failure replicates in all three non-augmented models** (75 dark / 222 bright tumours): detection gap VISTA-3D +0.58 [+0.47, +0.67], Universal Model +0.59 [+0.45, +0.69], D0 +0.72 [+0.62, +0.81]; Dice gap +0.34, +0.26, +0.38 (rule: GAP if the CI lower bound is above 0 and the point estimate at least 0.10; label GAP in detection and Dice for all three, in both drawn-phase strata). BRIGHT-50: detection -0.05 [-0.13, +0.02], Dice +0.10 [+0.00, +0.19]: INCONCLUSIVE (at the margin).
- **(c) The fix transfers to real arterial tumours:** change in gap BRIGHT-50 minus D0 on the arterial series (55 dark / 242 bright) is -0.66 [-0.78, -0.54] for detection and -0.22 [-0.29, -0.13] for Dice: CONFIRMED (detection and Dice). Detection among the bright arterial tumours **8.3% (D0) against 90.9% (BRIGHT-50)**. Single training runs, no seed-variance estimate.

**Exploratory result, not confirmatory: (b) the within-tumour sign effect.** The pre-registered direction of (b) was mis-specified (it predicted better detection on the phase where the tumour is brighter, contradicting prediction 1; PREREG Erratum 1). The pre-registered labels are reported as written (VISTA-3D INCONCLUSIVE, D0 INCONCLUSIVE, Universal Model OPPOSITE EFFECT, BRIGHT-50 REAL-PHASE BRIGHTNESS EFFECT), and the conditional-logit fits are fragile (about 70 discordant tumours, five covariates, 1500 to 2000 of 2000 bootstrap replicates needed the penalised fit; BRIGHT-50's label changes under four sensitivities). **(b) supports no claim.** Post hoc and descriptive only: in the 21 tumours that are bright on one phase and dark on the other with comparable magnitude, detection on the bright phase minus the dark phase is -0.29 (VISTA-3D), -0.52 (D0), -0.57 (Universal Model) and +0.05 (BRIGHT-50), the direction of prediction 1.

**Other findings.** nnU-Net's alignment-gate failures (D0 51 / 396 series, BRIGHT-50 47 / 396) are stray predicted-liver components, none a pipeline misalignment (`results/w4_waw/align_diag/ALIGN_DIAG.md`); the one series with a non-LAS orientation (LSP) was re-predicted on its LAS-reoriented CT for the nnU-Net models (DEVIATIONS 23; originals kept as a sensitivity; no verdict changes).

**Limits.** One centre, one disease (TACE-treated HCC, the same as HCC-TACE-Seg): a second-cohort replication, not generalisation across diseases. Masks were drawn on the phase where the tumour was most conspicuous (phase-selection confound, hence the strata and (b)). Propagated masks are registration estimates. VISTA-3D's training data cannot be fully excluded from the public record. D0 and BRIGHT-50 are single runs. The execution record (time-rule changes, idle GPU, an instance termination, watcher defects) is in DEVIATIONS 19 to 22.

**Reproduce.** Code and hashes: `results/w4_waw/code_hashes_w4.txt`; ground-truth build, prediction and scoring drivers `scripts/w4_waw_run_instance.sh` and `scripts/w4_waw_*.py`; analysis `python scripts/w4_waw_analyze.py results/w4_waw/paired_instances <out> results/w4_waw/outputs/primary/half0 results/w4_waw/outputs/primary/half1 --mask-check results/w4_waw/mask_check_cohort.json --host1-paired results/w4_waw/paired_host1`. The per-series scores and alignment files are committed (`results/w4_waw/outputs/`); the per-series predictions and ground-truth masks (about 0.4 GB) and the source scans (public, re-downloadable) are not in the repository.
